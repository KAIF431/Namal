CHAPTER 27

Chapter 27

⏱️ 6 Min Read • 1,016 Words • Author: Nimra Ahmed

Saare gul boote masnooi... rang, numoo, khushboo – dhoka hai.

Qasr ke sabzayzaar mein siyah shaam sunehray taaron ke saath jalwa gar hui thi. Bharpoor sajawat – siyah aur sunehri spray paint shuda asli gulaab, roshniyan, qahqahay.

Woh sab gol maizon ke gird kharay thay. Woh gol maiz itni oonchi thin ke seena tak aati thin. Kursiyan nadaarad. Ek maiz pe tag laga tha "Yousufs" aur uske gird wahi chaaron thay.

Sirf Haneen ka frock sunehri tha. Baqi Saadi aur Saim siyah suit mein thay aur Zummar ko toh siyah ki aadat thi. Woh be-ta'asur chehra liye, ghunghraali lat ungli par peetti samne dekh rahi thi. Siyah lambi qameez, kandhon par siyah hi dupatta. Baal khule thai.

Haneen ke baal magar French choti mein bandhe thay aur woh musalsal ird gird se guzarti ladkiyon ke pair dekh rahi thi. (Ameer ladkiyon ki shakalain jaisi bhi ho, pau balaa ke haseen hotay hain) Woh chehra ragad liyae jaate hain – pairon ka khayal daawaton mein hi aata hai.

Usne apne paon frock ke gher ke andar sametne ki nakaam koshish karte hue socha.

Saim kaafi purjosh aaya tha.

Haneen ne yeh keh kar ke:"Ammi ko baray abba ke paas chor detay hain, kyun phuppo?"

Zummar ki taa'id mili to Saadi inkaar na kar saka.

Same ko sabse zyada khushi somwaar ko apne doston ko apne ameer rishtedaron ki daawat ki tafseel batane ki thi.

Is liye raste mein baar baar woh dabi aawaz mein apna aur Kardar's ka rishta poochhta aaya tha:"Hashim bhai hamaray kya lagtay hain?"

"Dekho Saim! Hamare Nana ne do shaadiyan ki thi," Haneen ne pehli dafa tafseel se samjhaya."Pehli biwi se Ammi aur Waris Mamoo thay, jin ki biwi Sarah Khala hain. Pata hai na unka?"

Saim ne asbaat mein sar hilaya.

"Aur doosri biwi se Faaris Mamoo thai. Ab yeh jo doosri nani thi na, unke bhai Aurangzeb Kardar thay – Hashim bhai ke abbu."

"Yani Faris Mamu aur Hashim bhai first cousin huve?"

"Bilkul. Magar hamari Ammi ke first cousin nahi hain Hashim bhai – hamare woh kuch bhi nahi lagtay waise."

"Toh phir woh humein kaise jaantay hain?"

"Uff Saim! Khoon ka rishta nahi hai, magar Ammi ko souteli maan ke bheje huay to rishtedaar to lagay na. Ab dobara mat poochhna."

"Magar phir woh Zummar phuppo ko kaise jaantay hain?"

"Hashim bhai aur phuppo wakeel hain, ek saath kaam karte rahe honge, isi tarah shayad."

"To Hashim bhai ne Sarah Khala ko kyun nahi bulaya?"

"Uff mujhe kya pata? Sarah Khala to waise bhi ab kisi se zyada milti-julti nahi hain, aur humein bhi kabhi kabhi hi bulatay hain."

"Pehle kab bulaya tha? Mai toh kabhi nahi gaya." Saim ko toh gham lag gaya.

"Bas chand ek baar gaye thai hum unki taraf. Bhai aur mai. Ab chup kar ke baitho." Usne baat taal di aur mushkil se Saim ko khamosh karwaya.

Magar party mein aakar woh waqai khamosh ho gaya tha. Yeh uski duniya se mukhtalif duniya thi, aur usay bilkul bhi maza nahi aa raha tha.

"Yeh Hashim bhai aur kisi se hans kar baatein karte..." — Hashim ki taraf ishaara karte hue usne Haneen ke qareeb sargoshi ki — "Kitne artificial lagte hain na?"

"Ullu... ishaare mat karo!" Usne jaldi se Saim ka haath dabaya. Albata chehre ka rang badal gaya. Woh Hashim ko dekh bhi nahi pa rahi thi. Dil mein khauf alag — agar kisi ko pata chal gaya to?

Qasr-e-Kardar ke baahar...

Chand kilometers ke faaslay par ek veeran sarak par woh car khadhi thi. Chaand aur street pole ki mili juli madham roshni mein dekha to driving door se lake lagaye Faris Ghazi khada tha. Seene pe baazu lapet rakhe thay aur sar jhukaye zameen ko ragad raha tha.

Daftan usne sar utha kar dekha. Apni sunahri aankhein mutalashi andaaz mein daayein baayein ghumayi. Woh goya kisi ka muntazir lagta tha.

Us neem andheri jagah pe bhi uska chehra shafaf sa lagta tha. Jail wali pony ab kat chuki thi aur baal bohot chhotay ho chuke thay — goya ustura pher diya ho. Poori aasteen ki surmai shirt pehni hui thi. Wajeeha chehre pe bezari thi. Sunahri aankhein aur sustwan magar maghroor naak usay mazeed pur-kashish banati thi.

Woh waqai aisa mard tha jisko raah chalte bhi log mud kar ek do dafa toh zarur dekhte thai. Magar woh aam khoobsurat mardon ki tarah is baat se lutf andoz nahi hota tha.

Shayad ab nahi hota tha.

Ab uske chehre pe hamesha ek chidh chidha pan chhaya rehta tha — bezari aur gussa.

Balaakhir samne se ek car aati nazar aayi. Tez headlights ke bais Faris ne aankhein chundhiya kar muh pher liya. Headlights madham huvi, engine band hua, battiyan bujhi — sadak pe phir andhera chha gaya.

Darwaza khula aur ek naujawan bahar nikla. Yahan se uski pusht nazar aati thi. Sar ke baal siyah thay, neeche rough si jeans shirt pehni thi. Haath mein ek bag pakda tha.

"Ghazi!" — usne galay milne ko baazu aage badhaya aur Faris ne bag lene ko haath badhaya. Woh ruk gaya.

"Mubarak to dene de yaar."

"Mera samaan de de bas," woh khushk lehje mein bola — magar awaaz khushk nahi thi.

Naujawan ne afsos se sar jhatka, "Mai tumhara wahid dost hoon. Jail ka saathi raha hoon. Aur tumhein achhi tarah pata hai ke agar tumhein bahar laane mein Saadi ka haath hai to do chaar ungliyan meri bhi hai. Banda dosti ka hi lehaaz kar leta hai."

Bag saath dharte hue woh bohot narazi se bola tha.

"Samaan poora hai?" Usne zip khol kar dekha — taftishi, mashkook nigahon se ek ek cheez ko ulat pulat kiya. Andhere ke bawajood woh itna dekh sakta tha ke sab poora tha.

"Jaan par khel kar laya hoon yeh sab. Waise tum iska kya karoge?"

"Apni hifazat ke liye hai, aur kya karna hai?" — Woh ab bag ko car ki pichhli seat par rakh raha tha.

"Asleha apne liye bhi rakho to chalana dusre pe hi hota hai. Allah ko maano Ghazi. Abhi tum jail se nikle hi ho aur phir yeh kaam..."
