Chapter 155

⏱️ 7 Min Read • 1,229 Words • Author: Nimra Ahmed

Haneen, un kagazon ke dhair ke beech ab bhi baithi, mobile pe number mila rahi thi. Pehli dafa hickichat se, phir bechaini se, phir beqarari se, aur ab deewangi se baar baar Zumar ko number mila rahi thi. Aansoo uski aankhon se beh rahe the. Use laga, woh chand saal peechay chali gayi hai jab chhat par andhere mein baithe Zair ne naram lehje mein Sam aur use jinnaat ka qissa sunaya tha.

Tab use laga tha ke jinnat se zyada taqatwar insaan hota hai, aur uske liye woh insaan Zumar thi. jo uska har masla hal kar sakti thi. Ab bhi use yahi lag raha tha. Darmiyan ke mah o saal aur unki theh kahin kho gayi thi. Sirf Zumar thi, jise woh apna masla bata sakti thi.

Aur Zumar ne 7th call uthakar bas itna kaha.

"Haneen, main busy hoon. Tumhein zara der tak call karti hoon."

Aur woh khamosh aansuon ke saath phone haath mein liye baithi reh gayi. Kafi der baad phone baja. Usne dekha Zumar ka number aa raha tha.

Uski aankhon mein gussa utra, hatheli ki pusht se aankhein ragrein aur call uthai.

"Haan... Hannah sorry, main us waqt -" woh narmi se kehne lagi thi, magar Haneen ne drushti se baat kaat di.

"Sorry mujhe kehna chahiye. Ghalti se call kar li thi, kisi aur ko mila rahi thi. Bye."

Aur phone rakh diya. Aansoo phir se behne lage. Itne saal baad usne pehli dafa Zumar ko pukara tha, magar woh masroof thi. Kya uski masrufiyat Haneen ki bheegi, rundhi awaaz se zyada ahem thi? Uska dil toot sa gaya.

Zumar ki phir se call aane lagi, magar Haneen ne mobile off kar diya.

Alicia theek kehti thi. woh jald ya badir koi aisa gunah zaroor karegi, jiska kafara use poori zindagi dena padega. Bas Alicia ko yeh maloom nahi tha ke Haneen woh gunah chand mah pehle hi kar chuki thi.

____________________________

Hijr ki raat kaatne wale kya karega agar seher na hui?

Haneen ki adhuri, an-kahi call uske zehan mein aik si gai thi. Us subah bhi, woh samat khatam hote hi court room se nikalne ki bajaye kursi par baith gayi, aur abba ko call milane lagi.

Aaj dhoop nahi thi, aur sard kamra-e-adalat subah bhi battiyan jali thi. Justice sahab apne chambers mein wapas ja rahe the, ahlkaar Ahmer Shafiq naam ke ladke ko wapas le jane ki tayyari kar rahe the, Hashim phir nahi aaya tha, aur sab ka waqt hi zaya hua tha.

Woh aitaraf mein nazar daudati, Abba ko jati ghanti sun rahi thi.

"Aapne poocha Hannah se?"

Unka salam sunte hi, woh sar jhukaye, madham sa poochhne lagi.

"Maine call ki thi. Woh jaldi mein thi. Keh rahi thi ghalti se tumhein kar di thi call. Tum pareshan mat ho. Koi baat nahi hai."

"Unm-hmm. Koi baat thi. Woh theek nahi thi. Aap dobara poochhne ki koshish karein."

"Tum khud uske ghar chali jao."

Aur Abba ki taan yahin aakar toot jaati thi. Zamr ne "rehne dein, Abba" keh kar call kaati, toh ehsaas hua safed shalwar qameez mein koi uske saamne aa khada hua hai.

Chonk kar sir uthaya toh Ahmer tha. Uske Ammi bhi saath thi. Zumar ne idhar udhar dekha. Kamra khali ho raha tha.

"Ma'am!" Woh malalti, bechain sa, angrezi mein kehne laga. "Mujhe aap se baat karni hai."

"Apne wakeel ke bagair aapko mujhse baat nahi karni chahiye," narmi se kehti woh uthi. Purse kandhe par latkaya.

"Prosecutor Baseerat kahan hain? Mujhe unka poochhna hai."

Keh kar usne phir ahlkaaron se darkhwast ki ke chand lamhe mazeed usko baat karne de.

Woh ek hafte ki chhutti pe gaye hain. Woh mobile purse mein daalti jaane ko mudi.

"Mujhe Ghazi ke baare mein batata hai. Faris Ghazi, woh kuch galat karne ja raha hai."

Zumar ke qadam munjamid ho gaye. Aahista se usne gardan modhi. Aankhein sikud kar achanbe se use dekha.

"Kya?"

"Pehle aap wada karein ki kabhi zahir nahi karengi ki ye aapko mujhse maloom hua hai, warna Faris mujhe jaan se maar dega."

Pareshani se kehta woh aage ko hua.

"Main sun rahi hoon." Woh gaur se use dekhne lagi.

"Usne kuch plan kiya hai. Use adalat se umeed nahi rahi toh woh... jail mein kuch logo se inteqam lene ja raha hai. Woh kuch saathiyo ke saath jail mein riots karne ja raha hai. Aur is fasaad mein kuch log jaan se bhi jaayenge."

"Kya Faris ne khud kaha ye?"

"Ji. Ye woh tamaam tafseel hai jo mujhe maloom ho sakti hai. Woh mujhe bhi isme shaamil karna chahta hai magar maine abhi use hatmi jawab nahi diya."

Saath hi ek muda-tuda kaaghaz uski jaanib badhaya. Zumar ne kaaghaz pakad kar khojti nazron se use dekha.

"Aur aap mujhe ye kyun de rahe hain?"

"Mujhe police pe aitbaar nahi hai, kisi wakeel ko batana zyada behtar laga mujhe. Aap isko range haatho pakad sakti hain. Ab mujhe jaana chahiye."

Jaise koi iztirab khatam hua. Woh pursukoon sa saans leta ahalkaaron ke saath chala gaya. Zumar kaaghaz haath mein liye khadi, sochti nazro se us taraf dekhti rahi jahan se woh gaya tha.

Jab woh apni hawalati kothri tak wapas laya gaya toh subah pehar utar chuki thi. Sipahi ne salakhon ka darwaza khola, woh andar aaya toh darwaza maqfal kar diya gaya. Ahmer qadam-qadam chalta deewaar tak aaya aur phir farsh par akadu baith gaya.

Faris chand qadam door usi tarah baitha tha. Ahmer qareeb aaya toh usne noor se uske chehre ka jaiza liya.

"Kahan the?" Gardan mod kar use dekha jo qareeb baitha apne ghutno ko dekh raha tha.

"Kachehri."

"Maloom hai. Magar... kuch aur bhi hua hai kya?" Woh gaur se Ahmer ke chehre ko dekh raha tha.

"Wohi jo hona chahiye tha."

"Bak bhi chuko." Woh ukta gaya.

Ahmer ne hole se gardan mod kar use dekha.

"Maine unhein bata diya ki aap jail mein riots shuru karne lage hain."

Chand lamhon ke liye kothri mein sannata chha gaya. Fiza bojhal ho gayi.

"Aur? Usne yaqeen kar liya?" Faris ke poochhne pe Ahmer muskuraya.

"Ek ek harf pe." Aur uske haath pe haath maara. Dono halka sa hans diye.

Ye un chand dafa mein se tha jab Ahmer ne use haste dekha tha.

Madham awaaz mein kehne laga. Gada phir se sajidah hote hue, Faris ne jeb se muda-tuda kaaghaz nikala aur saamne phelaaya. Phir baahar dekha. Ahlkaar door the.

"Jumeraat ki raat faisle ki raat hogi. Agar usne yaqeen kar liya ki hum riots shuru karne lage hain, toh woh log jail ke shumaali hisse par idhar-"

Usne naqshay pe ek jagah ungli rakhi.

"Apni nafri teen guna badha denge. Aise mein, junubi mashriqi deewaar pe nafri kam ho jaayegi. Hum fasaad nahi karenge, hum sirf is taraf aag lagaayenge. Ye hamara diversion hoga, aur yun hum junubi mashriqi hisson se nikal jaayenge."

"Jaanta hoon. Hum koi teen sau dafa apna mansooba dohra chuke hain. Ab toh main khud ko aadha jail se baahar tasavvur karne laga hoon."

Wo ruka Faris, jo kaaghaz lapet raha tha, qadaray chonka.

"Ek minute. Tumhare chehre pe kuch aur bhi likha hai."

Usne gaur se Ahmer ko dekha.

"Koi masla hai kya?"

"Woh... Dar asal..."

Phir uth kar chand qadam door ja baitha, ki agali baat sunkar Faris Ghazi uska garebaan na jhapak le.

Kaan khujata hua, saadgi se bola,

"Prosecutor Basirat chhutti pe hain."
