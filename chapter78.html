"Haan! jurm wafaa dekhiye, kis kis pe hai  sabit,Woh saare khatadar sardaar khade hain."

Shehreen ne darwaza khatkhaya ya phir dhakal diya. Sheru couch pe aara tircha leta tha. Nigah pher kar bigde taa'surat ke saath usse dekha jo chokhat mein khadi thi. Bob cut sunhere baal, chonch ki tarah dono itraaf mein aage ko aate. Aankhon mein hamdardi thi.

"Mujhe afsos hai jo tumhare saath hua."  "Bohat shukriya." Usne sakhti se keh kar chehra pher liya. Phir chaunk kar wapas dekha. "Bhai ko toh nahi pata?"

"Mai bilkul bhi un logo mein se nahi hoon jo kisi ki pusht pe uski shikayat lagate hain. Mrs. Kardar ne bata diya ho toh woh alag baat hai"

Woh ungliyan baalo mein upar se neeche laate hue soch kar kehne lagi.

"Unko ek dam kaise pata chal gaya ke drugs tumhare kamre mein hi hogi?" "Lo, mummy ke liye chehre padhna kya mushkil hai. Tumhara chehra toh aate hi padh chuki thi mai, kayi dafa. Mai toh yeh soch kar hairaan hoon ke woh theek baithi thi study mein, phir achanak...? Tumhare dost ke jaate hi, unko kya ho gaya?"  Nosherwan ne chonk kar usse dekha.

"Saadi ke jaate hi?"

"Haan wohi tumhara dost, kaafi der betha raha tumahari mummy ke sath. Achhi gupshup hai uski, tumhari mummy se. Waha bhi uska zikar hota rehta na. Aaj ka toh aane ka programme bhi nahi tha. Yeh toh hum shaam ki chai pee rahe the jab mujhe koi message aaya. Shayad usi ka tha. Toh unhone foran aane ka kaha. Shayad koi zaroori baat hogi jis se Nami ko mutala karna zaroori hoga."

Bohat samajhdaar andaaz mein sir hilati woh wapas palti. Phir zara si mudh kar usse dekha. Aankhein sikar kar, kaafi hamdardi thi.

"Sheru, Tumhe nhi lagta ke tumhe apne jaiso se dosti karni chahiye. Kaha tum kaha woh."  Woh aur baahar chali gayi.

Nosherwan uljha uljha sa usay jaate dekhte raha. Phir ek dum utha. Shehreen ne kitchen se jhaank kar dekha. Woh kisi ke kamre ki taraf jaa raha tha. Woh pur sukoon si muskraadi.

"Sheru ke dost ka dakhla toh is ghar mein band hua."

Nosherwan andar aaya. Jawahirat haath room mein thi. Mobile bed side par pada tha. Usne ehtiyat se bath room ke darwaze ko dekha aur phone uthaya aur messages kholne laga. Saadi ke naam se message thai. Woh sar jhatakta phone rakhne laga. Phir kisi khayaal ke tehat rukha.

Bathroom ka darwaza ab bhi band tha. Woh phone haath mein liye chamakti screen par chand button aur dabane laga. Gmail kholi. Jawahirat ki mail... maane thi. Zara sa safha upar kiya aur reader par Saadi ka mail thread. Upar neeche tamam guftagu... Goya mukalma tha.

"Sheru kya kar raha hai aaj kal? Drugs toh nahi le raha? Kis se dosti hai? Drugs toh nahi le raha? Padhai kaisi jaa rahi hai? Drugs toh nahi le raha?"

Jawahirat ke taweel sawal aur Saadi ke mukhtasar jawab, lekin jawab bhi, behar haal jawab hote hain. Jaise jaise purane message khulte gaye, uska chehra khoon simat kar muskaraahat pe aata gaya. Ab bheench gayi.

Woh toliye se baal tehqiqati baahar nikle toh thithak kar reh gayi. Sheru ka laal bhabho ka chehra mobile ki light mein dikh raha tha. Woh toliya phenk kar aage aayi. Narmi se usay pukara.

"Kya dekh rahe ho?"  Usne shola baranga haath uthaya. Screen samne lehraayi. Jawahirat ne screen ko nahi dekha. Woh bechaini se uska chehra dekh rahi thi.

"Woh meri jasoosi karta tha aap ke liye?"  "Sheru, ab mai dobara drugs nahi loonga. Tum ne mujhse wada kiya tha."Usne Sheru ka baazoo hamaara.

"Nahi loonga nahi loonga. Kitni dafa batau? Magar usse main nahi chhodunga."Mobile bed pe phenk diya aur gusse se bahar nikal gaya. Jawahirat ne fauran phone uthaya aur Saadi ka number nikala. Call button pe haath rakha, phir ruk gayi.

"Woh drugs nahi lega, yeh tasalli thi toh doston ke aapas ke maamle mein usse padne ki kya zaroorat thi? Um hmm."  Shaane zara uchka kar usne phone door daal diya.

"Ab na woh main hoon, na tu hai, na woh mazi hai Faraaz jaise do saaye, tamanna ke saraabo mein mile."

Garma garam pakodo ki mehek saare mein pheli thi. Zumar apne makhsoos sofa pe baithi thi. Saim uske pairo ke qareeb carpet par bula ki jod tod raha tha. Haneen kaafi purjosh hi bartan laga rahi thi. Zumar ko dekhti toh sharma kar muskradeti. Woh bhi muskuradeti.

Faris abhi abhi aaya tha aur sirf salaam bola tha. Salaam mein bhi waqfa diya ke Zumar ki long dekh kar woh zara sa ruk gaya, phir remote uthakar channel badalne laga. Office se aaya tha, Coat tie sab fit tha.

"Yeh acchi lag rahi hai."Nudrat kitchen se idhar aayi toh sofa se kuch uthate huve Zumar ki badli hui long dekhi. Haneen, ne zara buland awaaz mein tabeer ki, plate lagaayi.

"Yeh phupho ko unki kisi purani student ne gift ki hai. Saath mein ek note bhi tha. Maine bhi padha tha note. Waise... Phupho! Aap ne uski likhai nahi pehchani? Mamu lein na."

Saath hi Mamu ko plate pakdaayi. Usne bina kisi taasur ke sanjeedgi se plate le kar side pe rakh di. Pakode abhi karahi mein thai.

"Nahi. Itna work hota hai, pehchana mushkil hota hai."Zumar saadgi se Nudrat ko qadray aahista awaaz mein bata rahi thi. Nudrat dobara kitchen mein aayi toh Haneen saath chali aayi aur kitchen mein khula darwaza band kar diya. Karahi mein pakode daalte hue Nudrat ne mudh kar usse dekha.

"Darwaza kyun band kiya?"

"Taake hero heroine se apne proposal par tabadla khayaal karle aur aap darmiyan mein entry na de."  Dhuaan lounge mein jaa raha tha. Extort chala kar aasteen modti, woh chatni banaane khadi ho gayi.

"Aaj tum us moyek computer aur Alicia ko chodh kar kitchen mein ghusi ho, hairat  hai."Ammi ki shikayat nazar andaz kar ke woh sar jhukaaye muskrate hue chutney ko chatne lagi.

Lounge mein TV ka shor tha ya Saim ki khud se ki jaane wali baatein.

"Mujhe aap se kuch baat karni thi, Faris!" Qadrey tazabzub se usne aaghaz kiya. Remote rakh kar rukh uski taraf kiya aur sanjeedgi se usse dekha.

"Kis silsile mein?"

"Ek case ke silsile mein."  Woh zara chonka. Usne samjha tha shayad... Uh hmm, yeh koi aur maamla tha.

"Aapko toh pata hai, baaz dafa ek wakeel istighasha mein hota hai aur aise faisle sunata hai jo doosre fareeq ke liye khushgawar nahi hote."Ruk ruk kar alfaaz adaa kiye. Faris ne sar hila kar saari baat decode ki.

"Yani aap ki wajah se kisi ko saza ho jati hai. Hmm phir?"  Wo zara der ko chup hui.

"Mere ek case ka faisla isi tarah hua tha. Mujrim ka bhai usse khush nahi tha aur woh iska izhaar bhi kar raha tha."
