Chapter 23

⏱️ 7 Min Read • 1,224 Words • Author: Nimra Ahmed

Aur Iblees ka saathi Mamoon bhi tha.Jannat se nikale jaane wali ek kamtar rooh - Farebkaar,Ke wahan bhi uski nigah aur soch neeche jhuki rehti aur zyada sarahati sone ki bani jannat ki rosh ko.Yeh manzar se kisi bhi doosray se zyada maza deta tha.

Ussi ne sikhaya bani no-e-insaan koApne na-paak haathon se dharti maa ke batan ko khod kar lootna un khazanon ko jo chhupe behtar thay.Jald hi uski fauj ne jahannum ki pahaari mein dala ek wasee chheed,Aur khod daale sone ki pasliyaan.

Na ho koi hairaan is baat peKe sona ugta hai andher jahannum mein,Ke shayad mitti hi qabil hai is qeemti balaa ke.

(Makhooz az Milton - Jannat Gumshuda)

Husn o ishq ka soz ta'alluq simton ka paband nahi hota.Aksar toh khud shama ka shola barh ke gaya parwane tak.

Hashim Kardar ki beti Sonia ki siyah sunahri salgirah aaj, yaani haftay ki shaam ko thi.Shayad isi liye haftay ki subah bhi chamkeeli sunahri tuloo hui thi.

Zulfiqar Yousuf ke ghar mein nashta ka dhuwan, Nudrat ki daant bhari takeeden, Haneen ki bhaag-daud bhari tayyari - sab ek saath chal raha tha.Saadi aaj subah-subah restaurant chala gaya tha.Saim ab uniform mein tayyar gol mez ke gird baith kar nashta kar raha tha.Haneen apna siyah coat aur shoes polish karke jab aayi toh uski plate ko dekh kar muh ban gaya:

"Ammi... main nahi khaunga dhakkan toast. Yeh mota aalu mere liye bread ka pehla aur aakhri toast hi bachata hai hamesha!"Woh maathay ke curly baalon par brush pherti wahi se chillayi.

Kitchen se Nidrat ka dupatta hua jawab foran aaya:"1000 dafa kaha hai khaane ki cheezon ke naam mat rakha karo!"

Usne muh mein burburaate hue aagay ho kar Saim ka aadha paratha uthaya aur tod liya. Khilaf-e-maamool Saim ne koi rad-e-amal zahir na kiya. Chup chaap kha raha tha.

Woh nashta kar ke uthi thi ke Saim ne pukara,"Nah nah Haneen...?"Usne ghoor kar usay dekha, "Tareekh gawah hai ke tum ne mujhe baghair kaam ke 'Dumba' kabhi nahi kaha."

"Aaj ka lunch meri taraf se hai." Haath jhaadhte hue usne mazeed sanjeedgi se ittila di.Haneen ne bag kandhay par dala, file uthai aur istehzaai andaaz mein sar jhatka,"Mujhe guess karne do ke kya mangwaya ho ga tum ne... Haan haan, samosay, saath mein charga aur aaloo ke chips. Aur jaise in sab ashya pe..."

La'anat bhej kar woh darwazae ki taraf barh gayi jahan baahar van waala horn de raha tha.

"Spring rolls, Bihari kabab, aur baked huay aaloo," Saim ne aqab mein baray sukoon se kaha.Haneen ke qadam zanjeer hue, aankhein yaqeen se phel gayi. Yakdam kohni se daboch kar usay saamne khada kiya."Phir saath mein hogi pudine ki chutney?" Aur mashkook nazaron se ghoora.

"Uh-huh... tumhari favourite mayonnaise wali sauce!"

Haneen ke lab bharpoor muskurahat mein phel gaye. Aankhon mein shararat chamki. Baazu chhoda aur chalne ka ishaara kiya."Ab kaam batao."

"Raat ko Hashim bhai ki beti ki salgirah hai, mujhe bhi jaana hai," woh dono saath chalte baahar aaye to bagheecha cross karte hue Saim ne kaha."Saadi bhai ne kaha tha ke Ammi nahi ja rahi to main ghar mein rahoon."

"Hmm. Tumhare paas black suit hai?""Haan, wahi jo bhai ne birthday par diya tha.""Toh phir usay dhoop lagwa lo, hawa lagwa lo aur istrii karwa lo," woh gate band kar ke van ki taraf badhte hue bade sukoon se boli.

Saim ne khushgawaar be-yaqeeni se usay dekha, "Magar tum bhai ko kaise manao gi? Woh to sorry bhi nahi..."

"Saim Yousuf! Ye jo aaj tum mujh pe apni pocket money choonk rahe ho na, ye is liye hai ke tumhein pata hai is kaam ke liye sahi bandi main hi hoon. Is liye apne suit ki fikar karo bas!" Keh kar woh van mein chadh gayi.

Andar Rafia aur Khadija buri tarah dohrayi karne mein magan thein. Jabke Naima kitaab kholay kuch likh rahi thi. Aaj unka aakhri paper tha.

"Kaisee tayari hai?" Usne imtihaan ki subah ka maqsoos sawal dohraya."Yaar! Kuch nahi aata. Samjho sab mix up ho gaya." Rafia ne harasaan nafi mein sar hilate hue maqsoos jawab diya.

Haneen ne apni file kholi aur sarsari si nigah dauraayi. Phir kisi ehsaas ke tehat Naima ko dekha... Woh tissue paper pe kachi pencil se likh rahi thi.

Naqal ke yeh tareeqay in ko jaane soojhtay kahan se the."Agar pakri gayin to?" Haneen ne qareeb ho kar sargoshi ki.Usne ghoor kar usay dekha.

"Toh garmi garmi kartay us se paseena ponch loongi. Saare saboot khatam!" Usne shanay uchka diye to Haneen sar jhatak kar apna padhne lagi.

Saim khidki se baahar dekhta apne suit aur un doston ke baare mein soch raha tha jin ko usne Somwaar ki party ki tafseelat deni thi. Zehan mein woh fikray tarteeb de raha tha - "Pata hai hamaray aik uncle hain... uhun cousin hain... Hashim bhai! Un ka ghar pata hai kaisa hai..."Saim ko ye soch kar hi maza aa raha tha ke ah, kitne maze se apne doston ko saare qissay sunaye ga.

---

"To ne kya kiya ae zindagi, dasht o dar mein phiraya mujheAb to apne dar o baam bhi jaante hain paraya mujhe"

Kardar khandaan ke qasr ke sabzaar mein mulazimon ka amla aur fazil waiters party ki tayyariyon mein masroof the. Andar lounge mein bhi safai suthrai ka amal jaari tha.Shehrein motazalil qadamoun se zine chadhti upar ja rahi thi. Hashim ka kamra sunsaan pada tha. Woh aagay badhi. Noshervan ke kamray ka dakhli darwaza khula tha aur aagay balcony ka bhi. Woh balcony mein baitha tha - laptop god mein, kaanon mein earphones.

Shehrein wahi khadhi rahi... yahaan tak ke Noshervan ne chonk kar us taraf dekha, to woh sar jhatak kar jaane lagi.

"Aap kab aayi? Aaiye," Shero jaldi se earphones nikalte hue utha. Uska chehra khil utha tha. Us roz ki nisbatt aaj durust mijaaz mein tha.Woh usay pasand karta tha, koi andha bhi bata sakta tha. Aur Shehrein andhi nahi thi. Albattah usay maloom tha ke woh kehne ki himmat nahi rakhta.

Shehrein ne pareshani mein nafi mein sar hilaya, "Nahi, tum baitho."Phir ruki -"Hashim hai yah...?" Usne Noshervan ke bhai ka naam liya. Wohi bhai jiske darr ke baais Shero kabhi keh nahi saka.

"Bhai ka off tha, magar woh shayad Shehla aunty ke case ke liye kahin gaye hain. Unke driver ne accident kar diya tha kisi ka."

Woh abhi tak muntazir khadha tha. Shehrein ki aankhon mein mayoosi ubhri."Khair, woh hota bhi to mera kaam nahi hona tha... it's okay. Jaane do." Woh keh kar palatne lagi.

"Kya kaam? Mujhe batayein." Woh qadam qadam uthata us tak aaya."Chodho... tum se nahi hoga."

"Well! Agar aap ne apne kaam ka zikar mujh se kiya hai to yaqeenan aap ko lagta hoga ke main kar sakta hoon... to bataayein."

"Woh itna bewakoof bhi nahi tha..."

Shehrein thake andaaz mein muskurayi, "Sonia... wohi hai asal masla. Usko meri aur Hashim ki honeymoon ki pictures chahiye."

"Toh aap ke paas nahi hain?" Noshervan ko andar se shayad khushi hui."Main takleef deh yaadon ko sambhal kar nahi rakhti," usne sunahray baalon mein haath pher kar unhein peechay karte hue kaha. Woh dono hanuuz balcony par kharay the.

"Shaadi ki to mere paas bhi hongi.""Magar honeymoon wali Hashim ke laptop mein hongi. Aur main tumhare bhai ke mooh nahi lagna chahti," usne bohot hi la-parwai se laptop ka zikar kiya.
