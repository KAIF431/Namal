Chapter 37

⏱️ 7 Min Read • 1,088 Words • Author: Nimra Ahmed

Go ke Hashim yahi chahta tha, magar Nosherwan ka paara ki tarah Tezz chadhta gussa karne ke liye usay uski kohni thaamna padi. Nosherwan sar jhatak kar rukh mod gaya. Shehreen bas zabt se in dono ko dekhti ja rahi thi.

"Ainda mere khilaaf kisi ki madad karne se pehle yeh soch lena ke phir tumhe saari zindagi apni beti ki shakal nahi dekhne dunga. Aur agar koi shak ho to pehli qist tum teen din baad tab dekhogi jab tum chhuttiyon par Dubai akeli jaogi. Sonia ko is liye chodh raha hu ke do din guzar lo us ke saath."

Shehreen ke ta’asuraat badle — bechaini, pareshani. Woh tezi se aage badhi.

"Hashim! Sonia mere saath jaayegi. Yehi tay hua tha."

"Tay karne wala main tha, mansookh bhi main kar raha hoon."Muskuraahat gayab thi aur woh darshti se chaba chaba kar keh raha tha.

"Talaaq ke waqt apni beti maine tumhare hawale ki ke tum maa thi. So maine tum par ehsaan kiya tha. Tab se hafte mein do din apni beti ko le jaata hu. Baaki woh tumhare saath rehti hai. Tumhein meri taraf se koi pareshani nahi milti. Aur is sab ka sila tum ne meri pusht par waar kar ke diya."

Uski awaaz unchi hoti ja rahi thi. Nosherwan ab zara kam gusse se unko dekh raha tha. Andar se pareshani bhi thi — Shehreen beti ke bagair kaise rahegi?

"Main Sonia ke bagair kaise rahoon gi? Tum yeh nahi kar sakte."Uska saara ton-tona jhaag ban kar baith gaya.

"Yeh to pehle sochne wali baat thi. Do din guzar lo, aur teesre din meri beti ko wapas chodh jao. Aur yeh to tum jaanti hi ho ke meri beti ko meri marzi ke bagair tum duniya ke kisi mulk le jaaogi to main uss mulk se bhi nikalwa sakta hu."

"Usne sirf password manga tha. Use woh wapas chahiye tha jo tum ne usse liya tha. Mujhe nahi pata woh kis cheez ki baat kar raha tha. Tum mere saath yun mat karo Hashim."

Hashim chonka. Phir sar jhatka.

"Nahi pata tha to uski madad kyun ki? Tumhari beti ka baap hoon mai, aur yeh tumhari beti ka chacha hai jisko tum ne use kiya. So ab tum Sonia ko nahi le kar ja rahi ho."Qat’i andaaz mein keh kar woh mur gaya. Dono tezi se car tak wapas aaye. Darwaze jhat se khule. Shehrin khadi rahi — bebasi, pareshani se lab kaat'ti.

"Maine Saadi ko underestimate kiya tha."Hashim baithte hue barbaaraaya.Nosherwan ne be-ikhtiyaar use dekha.

"Matlab?"

"Kya tum sun nahi rahe thay? Use woh chahiye tha jo maine usse liya tha — Waris ke laptop ke documents. Woh mere paas thai."Kehte hue chauffeur ko ishaara kiya. Woh sar hila kar driving seat ki taraf aaya.

"Haan… magar pandrah minute mein woh kitne documents padh sakta hai?"

"Shayad ek bhi nahi… magar pandrah minute mein woh un sab ko copy zarur kar sakta hai."Keh kar Hashim jaise saari duniya par laanat bhej kar khidki se baahar dekhne laga.

Nosherwan khamosh ho gaya.Usse Shehreen ki haalat dekh kar khushi nahi huvi thi. Shehreen ka kasoor nahi tha. Yeh Saadi tha jo har cheez ke darmiyan aaya tha.Uska kasoorwar hamesha Saadi hi nikalta tha.

____________________________

“Hum hi nahi thay… humari tarah ke aur bhi log azaab mein thay jo duniya se alag sochte thay.”

Subah ki sunehri safedi mein garmi ki haddat barhti ja rahi thi. Marhoom Zulfiqar Yousuf ke ghar mein chalte Amar Cooler ne TV wale kamre ko qadray thanda kar rakha tha. Nudrat idhar udhar bikhri cheezen samet rahi thi. Saath saath raahdari ki gol mez par baithe Haneen aur Usama ka lecture bhi jaari tha.

“Itna nahi hota ke jo cheez uthao usay jagah par rakho?”

“Ammi! Main sab kuch jagah par wapas rakhta hoon,” Saim ne ehtijaaj kiya.

“Ji, magar kisi aur ki jagah par,” Haneen ne baat mukammal ki. Woh saath mein chai bhi pee rahi thi.

“Tum toh jaise sab theek rakhti ho na! Abhi tumhari almaari kholon to kapdon ka Mount Everest neeche gir jaaye.”

“Aur jaise tum us Mount Everest talay dab kar zakhmi ho jaoge,” usne sukoon se doosra ghoont bhara. Aaj french choti banane ki zehmat nahi ki thi. Khulay baal seedhay magar zara bikhrey huve thai.

Nudrat, mureedaan dono ko kuch kehay baghair raahdari se guzarti hui Saadi ke kamre tak gayi. Itna toh woh dekh chuki thi ke woh Fajr tak kaam karta raha tha, phir so kar 9 baje uth gaya. Bed par baithe joggers ke lace baandh raha tha. Nudrat ne pyaar se usay dekha. Woh bada ho gaya tha… aur lamba bhi. Magar uske chehray par ek no-umar larkon wali masoomiyat ab bhi thi.

Woh seedha hua to maa ko khadhe paaya. Suti huvi aankhon se muskuraya.

“Kya baate huvi bade Abbu se?”Woh uth kar laptop bag mein jamaane laga.

“Wahi unki purani fikr… Zumar ki shaadi.”Unho ne thaki huvi saans kheenchhi.Saadi khamoshi se cheezen samet ta raha.

“Woh usko samjha samjha kar thak gaye hain, magar woh nahi maanti. Saadi! Tum samjhao na… ab toh tumhari baat cheet hoti hai phupo se. Aur tumhari baat toh woh hamesha maanti hai.”

Saadi ne bag ka strap kandhay par dala. Chehray par chhupay huzn ko chhupane ki nakaam koshish karte huve kuch kehne laga tha ke phone baj uthaa. Jaise jaan bach gayi. Nudrat baat bhool kar wapas chali gayi, aur usne anjaana number utha liya.

“Milna hai mujhe isi waqt. Kidhar aau?”Faris ke alfaaz bhi usi ki tarah hotay thay — theek theek theek.

“Main to nikal raha tha… restaurant aa jao.”Usne darmiyani raasta nikala.

“Aadhe ghante tak,” aur phone band.

“Par mamu bhi na… aagay peechay ki baat nahi karenge kabhi.”Usne muskara kar sar jhatka. Phir Nudrat ki baatein yaad aayi.

Phupo kya bhi uski maanti thi?“Uhn uhn…”Woh bahar aaya to Haneen haath hila kar purjosh hi Saim se keh rahi thi:

“Aur itne baday baday khulay lawns, Saim! Tumhara dil nahi chahta ke humara bhi itna bada ghar ho aur khoob daulat ho humare paas bhi? Nahi, yeh nahi ke hamara chhota ghar mujhe bura lagta hai, yeh sab bhi acha hai… magar zyada bara ghar. Soncho Saim!”

Sakm ne peeche se Saadi ko aate dekh liya tha, so jawab nahi diya. Usko sahi jawab maloom hi na tha.

“Tum toh ho hi kuen ke mendak, tumhe kya pata…”Lekin woh afsurdah hui.

“Agar main yeh baat apni kisi dost se karti to woh kehti ke ‘lalach buri bala hai’. Kya zyada paisay ki khwahish hona buri cheez hai?”
