"Toh aap ne chhutti khatam hone se pehle aane ki zahmat kyun ki?"

"Pehle main baith jaaun?"Usne poocha.

Woh usi tarah khafgi se usay dekhti rahi. Saadi phir se baitha aur bundle uski taraf badhaya.

"Aap ka kaam waqt se pehle kar diya hai. Field pe jaane ki saari tayari bhi mukammal kar li hai. Ab aap woh shikayat bataayein jo aap ko mujh se hai."

Sarah ne file band ki. Taik lagayi aur sanjeedgi se usay dekha.

"Tumhein pata hai, Saadi! Thar ke is field pe hazaron log kaam kar rahe hain. Aur in sab ke upar is ohday par pohanchne wali main waahid aurat hoon. Aur iski wajah maloom hai kya hai?"

Saadi ne sar hilaya.

"Mere jaise zaheen aur qabil senior engineer ka saath hona?"Saadi ki zubaan phisli.

"Nahi. Apne kaam se committed ho kar rehna aur bila wajah ke naghon se parheiz karna."

"Aap ko pata hai main bila wajah chhuttiyan nahi karta. Ab bhi kayi kaam thai..."To woh khamosh ho gaya aur sanjeeda bhi.

"Itne ahem kaam ke tumne mujhe Faaris ke raha hone ka nahi bataya?"

"Aap ne poocha hi nahi."Usne saadiqi se shanay achkaaye.

"Poocha tha maine! Tumne to baat taal di thi."

"Acha na... Ab toh pata chal gaya aap ko." Woh khushgawar andaz mein guftagu ki noiyat badalne laga.

Sarah ab fikarmandi se usko dekh rahi thi.

"Tum bohot pur-israr hotay ja rahe ho. Ab to kuch batate hi nahi ho."

"Har cheez ka ek waqt hota hai. Maine kaha tha na, is bande ke laptop tak pohanch jaunga. Phir..."

"Kaun hai woh?"

Kya usne Waaris ko saare shikwe bhool kar—Sarah ne aage hote ihtiyaat se poocha.

Saadi ne isbaat mein sir hila diya.

"Bas thoda sa intezar kar lein aur yeh sab mujhe sambhalne dein."

Muskura kar bashaasht se kehta woh uth khada hua.

Sarah ki aankhon mein shikayat phir se aud kar aayi.

"Ladke, tum agle mahine mujhe field pe apne saath chahiye ho. Tayari kar lo."

"Right... Boss!" Muskura kar maatha tak haath le ja kar salaam kiya aur jaane ko mud gaya.

Sarah ne mushkil se muskurahat dabaye sar jhatka.

"Yeh Saadi bhi na..."

"Yeh hain ahl-e-duniya ke dilchasp dhokay, kisi ko kisi se mohabbat nahi hai."

Nausherwan shooting point par khada tha.

Uski lane mein ek patla phadphada raha tha.

Usne dono haathon se pistol pakre, bazu seedhe kiye. Ek aankh band kiye nishana baandha.

Kanon pe pehle hi headphone type air protection pehne hue the aur aankhon par zard glasses.

Taak kar usne fire kiya.

Ek, do, teen, chaar. Sab dil ke aas paas lage. Dil tootne aur phatne se bacha raha.

"Haath seedha rakho. Kandhe mat jhinko. Is point ko dekho. Apne qareeb..."

Hashim ki madham awaaz sun kar woh chonk gaya.

Glasses lagaye, woh mud kar dekhnay laga...

Hashim usko dekhe bina aage ho kar uske haath ko seedha kar raha tha. Nausherwan ne hole se sar jhatka, bezari zahir karne ki koshish ki.

Magar chunanchay woh Hashim ki amad se bezar nahi hua tha, yeh kaam raha. Uska baazu seedha kar ke Hashim peeche hata.

"Hoon... Ab nishana lo, poori yaksooi se."

Uske kandhe ke peeche khade hue woh pehlu ko dekh kar bola.

Nausherwan ne pehlu ko dekha, palkain sukedhi, gehri saans andar kheechi aur fire kiya.

Putle ka dil ab bhi nahi phata.

Woh ukta kar sar jhatakta ek taraf ho gaya. Machine ne woh putla peeche kar ke fresh patla saamne kiya.

Hashim uski jagah pe aa khada hua. Pistol ka upari hissa peeche kar ke load kiya.

"Shehreen na itni khoobsurat hai, na itni mutasir jo ke tum abhi tak is sadme se bahar nahi nikle."

Dono haathon mein pakda pistol taak kar nishanay par rakhte woh bola.

"Woh aap ki biwi rahi hai."

Shero sar jhuka kar jootay se farsh masalne laga. Woh is mawzoo se bachna chahta tha.

"Mujhe is se farq nahi padta. Tum batao, tumhari woh pasand thi, mohabbat thi ya ishq tha?"

Saamne dekhte hue Hashim ne fire kiya.

Goliyon ki tar tarahat shooting range ke is andarooni kamray mein goonji.

Yake baad deegaray do goliyaan putlay ke dono haathon pe lagin.

"Is se kya farq padta hai?"

Sheero ne bezari se shanay achkaaye.

"Farq padta hai. Agar yeh pasandedgi thi to shaam tak tumhein theek ho jaana chahiye."

Kehte hue usne phir fire kiya.

Dono aankhon ke beech goli ne surakh kar diya.

"Agar mohabbat thi to kuch din lagenge."

Zor daar goonj ke saath agli goli peshaani pe mari.

"Aur agar ishq tha to—"

Pehle ka nishana leye nazron ke saamne surkh rumal sa lahraaya. Red. Surkh.

"To phir yeh la ilaaj hai."

Aakhri goli. Dil pe mari. Dil phat gaya.

Hashim ne gala mazaa taara, aankhein sikeer kar tanqeedi nigahon se pehle ka jaiza liya, jise ab peeche le jaya ja raha tha.

Alamati tor par pistol ki naali pe phoonk maari.

Use pant ki pichli jeb mein arsa aur pur sukoon sa Nausherwan ki taraf mud gaya.

"Pasand se zyada, mohabbat se kam."

Woh jootay se musalsal farsh masal raha tha.

"Ya shayad Shehreen ke tumhein istemaal karne se zyada sadma tumhein Saadi ke kehne par istemaal kiye jaane pe hua hai."

Nausherwan ke jhuke chehre pe maara ehaanat ke surkhiyan dorne lagin. Muthiyan bheench lein.

Hashim ne bohot ghoor se use dekha.

"Saadi ko duniya mein sab se zyada mohabbat kis se hai, maloom hai?"

Nausherwan ne sulagti nigahain utha kar use dekha.

"DA Zamar se?"

Hashim ne asbaat mein gardan hilaayi.

"Aur uski nazar mein hum use gira chuke hain. Unke kharab taluqaat muklis baramdgi ke baad mazeed kharab ho jaayenge. Jald Saadi mere paas aayega aur main apne tareeke se usko sambhal loonga."

"Agar woh mere liye kaam karne lag jaye to socho, hamara ghulaam ban kar humein kitna faida dega."

"Woh kabhi hamara ghulaam nahi banega. Namumkin!"

Aur itna to Nausherwan usay jaanta hi tha.

"Main usay andekhi zanjeeron mein jakadh loonga, Shero! Ek din woh mere liye kaam karega. Uska talent hamare haq mein istemaal hoga!"
