"Yani usne aap ko dhamkiyaan waqara di hain. Hmm, aage?"

"Ahm. Jee Aap jaante hai hamare khandaan mein..."

"Aap mamla ghar tak nahi le jaana chahti, bahar hi bahar hal karna chahti hain."Is dafa fiqrha hi nahi poora hone diya. Woh gehri saans lekar ruk gayi.

"Main chahti hoon ke yeh mamla mai...' Woh ruk gayi. Baat lapet kar karne ka faida nahi tha. Woh shakhs school ki teacher nahi tha jisse woh aitmad se peecha chuda sakti thi.

"Agar mai aap ke department mein iski shikayat darj karwau toh us shakhs ki rokne ka tareeqa kya hoga?"

"Koi masla nahi"

Woh peeche ho kar baitha. Kaane ki aur gaddte hue laa parwai se shaane uchkaaye.

"Main director se baat kar lunga. Hamari van usay pick kar legi. Do chaar haath lagenge toh dimag durust ho jaayega uska."

Zumar ki aankhein beyaqeeni se phel gayi. Foran nafi mein sir hilaaya.  "Nahi, please. Mai tashadud par yakeen nahi rakhti. Yeh masla baat cheet se hal ho sakta hai. Sab ke andar achayi ka asar hota hai. Humein sirf uska izhaar karne ki zarurat hoti hai."

"Aap do ghante ke liye usay mere ladko ke hawale karde. Saari andar ki achayi baahar aa jaayegi."Phir uske taa'surat dekh kar woh ruk gaya. Isse pehle ke woh usay yeh zikar bhool jaane ka keh deti, woh qadray narmi se bola.

"Thik hai. Baat kar lete hai phir. Mai mil loonga usse. Mard ka baat karna aur hota hai."

"Okay!"Usne sar hilaaya. Zara tasalli hui.

"Woh aadmi aaj kal court aata hai, roz apeel ke chakkar mein. Agar aap subah aa jayein toh mai usay dikha dungi."

"Sure! Koi aur masla?"

"Nahi bas yahi tha. Thanks!"Woh halka sa muskrayi. Faris ne ghari dekhi aur awaaz di"Haneen, laa rahi ho yaa mai jau?"

"Nahi, la rahi. Aap jaye,"Woh dish uthakar aati hui bade mood mein boli. Aaj woh bohot khush nazar aa rahi thi.

__________________________

"Khaali haatho ko kabhi ghour se dekha hai Faraz... Kis tarah log lakeeron se nikal jaate hai."

Pakode khatam ho gaye. Zumar chali gayi. Ammi namaz padhne kamre mein gayi toh Faris unke paas chala gaya. Ab Haneen thi aur online.

"Mera masla hal ho gaya."Usne chehkate hue ittila di. Alicia aadatan hasi.

"Himmat ki ya zyada himmat wala dhoonda?"

"Zyada himmat wali ko dhoondh kar kuch himmat kar li."

Phir khayal aane pe cellphone uthakar dikhaya.

"Yeh dekho, mujhe gift mila."

"Wow! Brand new?"Woh bhi pur-josh hi aage hokar dekhne lagi.

"Haan aur bhi bohot kuch hai. Ek ameer se uncle hai hamare ahbaab mein."Woh collar jhaad kar boli.

"Waqai? Aur woh kaun hai?"  "Mere uncle ke uncle. Yeh pechida rishtedariyaan tum nahi samjhogi. Achha, mujhe ek baat batao, tumne us travel's wali game mein.."

Landline phone ki ghanti pe woh badmaza hui Aage badh kar number dekha. Bade abba ke ghar se tha. Dusri ghanti par woh khamosh ho gaya. Ammi ne andar se uthaal liya hoga. Woh mutmain hi hokar baat karne lagi. Phir ek dum ruki. Jaldi se Alicia ko 'bye' kaha aur aahista se receiver uthakar kaan se laga liya. Hassab-e-twakku badi ammi hi thi. Woh chamakti hui aankhon ke saath sunne lagi.

"Maine toh pehle hi bata diya tha, Zumar nahi manegi. Usne toh saaf inkaar kar diya hai."

"Magar mein khud baat karke dekhun, shayad..." Nudrat ko ab bhi aas thi.

"Bhai, jab usne inkaar kar diya toh kya gunjaish reh gayi? Dekho, bura na maano woh usay jaanti hai. Uska kehna hai ke woh mizaj ka bohot sakht aur gusse wala hai, wild sa. Uske saath kaise guzara karegi woh?"

Haneen ne receiver rakh diya. Uska chehra bilkul zard pad gaya tha. Bara baj gaye thai aur Cinderella ki sawari jo woh udti ja rahi thi. Badsoorat kaddu me badal kar zameen bosh huvi thi.

Nudrat ko aam taur pe extension se doosra phone uthaye jaane ka pata chal jata tha ke awaaz halki ho jaati thi, lekin aaj nahi chal saka. Unhone be-basi mein samne baithay Faris ko dekha, jo baghoran ke taasur padh raha tha aur receiver cradle par daal diya.

"Inkaar kar diya?"

"Mai Zumar se khud baat karungi. Woh is tarah ki baat nahi keh sakti. Woh..."

"Kis tarah ki baat? Keh de, Mai bura nahi manunga."

"Yeh hi gussa aur mizaj ki bakhti. Magar tum is baat ko ego ka masla na banana. Mujhe ek dafa mazeed..."

"Nahi, koi zarurat nahi. Inkaar ho gaya, baat khatam."

"Faris sirf ek dafa mujhe.."Woh nafi mein sir hilaata uth khada ho gaya.

"Aapa banda izzat se rishta maangta hai, aur izzat se na mile toh khissa tamam. Mai das saal ka tha jab mera baap faut ho gaya tha. Umar guzri hai rishtedaaro ki siyasat dekhte dekhte. Yeh sautele ka lafz aakar khatam huva jab hum ne ek doosre ko samajhna shuru kiya, shayad das baarah saal pehle. Warna is se pehle Waris ho, aap ho ya aap logon ke rishtedaar, mai sab ke liye doosri biwi se hone wala sautele beta hi tha aur aap mein aap se koi mujhe pasand nahi karta tha. Mai yeh sab aapka dil dukhane ke liye nahi keh raha. In baato ki ab koi ehmiyat nahi. Bas itna bata raha hoon ke mai aap ke rishtedaaro mein agar shaadi karta toh izzat se karta warna nahi,. Is liye ab dobara unse baat mat ki jiye ga."

Nudrat ne aahista se sar asbaat mein hilaaya. Woh usko samajh sakti thi.
