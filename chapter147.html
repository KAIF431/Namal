"Hashim Kardar?" Zumar ko shock se nikalne mein chand lamhe lage aur phir ek dum aankhon mein nagwari dikhayi di.

"Uska naam kaise le sakte ho tum?"  "woh unke cousin hain, phir jaidad ka tanazaa hai! Woh Faris Ghazi ko isme fasa sakte hain. Isse unko faida hoga nuqsan nahi."

"Okay Saadi, bohot ho gaya." Usne taang par rakhi doosri taang seedhi ki aur drushti se kehne ko aage huyi, "mein yeh defense strategy bohot dafa court mein istemal chuki hoon. Jab apne difa mein koi baat na ho, toh kisi teesre shakhs par shak dalwa do. Magar, kya tumhare paas koi saboot hai?"

Saadi ki gardan nafi me hili. Kya is audio aur un tasaveer ka Hashim ke computer se milna aisa saboot tha jo woh pesh kar sake? Hargiz nahi

"Phir tum kaise kisi pe itna bada ilzaam laga sakte ho? Faris ke khilaf meri gawaahi ko chhod do, jab bhi saboot hain. Uski gun, uske fingerprints... Tum mujhe usse bade saboot Hashim ya kisi aur ke khilaf laakar do, main tumhari baat sunungi. Magar isse pehle nahi."    Talkhi se bolti woh khadi ho gayi. Saadi ne gardan utha kar use dekha. Woh uktayi hui lag rahi thi.

"Toh aap dhai saal se hamari baat isliye nahi sun rahi kyunki hum saboot nahi de rahe?"

"Agar mujhe jhoota kehne ki jagah kuch aur kehte, toh main sunti."

"Aap apni jagah theek hain." Sar hila kar woh khada ho gaya. Chand lamhe dono aamne samne khade rahe.

"Aakhri baat, Phupho." Woh zara jhijhak kar bola, "Mujhe kisi aise wakeel ka bataiye jo hum afford bhi kar sakein aur wo hamare saath mukhlis bhi ho.... Faris Ghazi ke liye." Woh ruka. Uske samne wo Ab use mamu kehne se danista aitraaz baratne laga tha.

Zumar ne sar jhatka. Zara tauqqaf kiya. Tane asab dheele jaise pade.  "Khilji sahab se mil lo. Unka number aur pata text kar deti hoon. Unke pehle taa'sur par mat jana, ache wakeel hain."  Aur usi tarah seene par bazu bandhe wo mud gayi. Use peeche aane ka nahi kaha.

Chahe to wo andar aa jaye chahe to na aaye. Saadi yasiat se use jaate dekhta raha. Dhai saal se woh sirf uski phupho thi. Zumar nahi. Agar ek dafa, ek dafa woh shikwa kar de, toh woh use bata dega... Ya shayad nahi. Bas ek dafa..

Jo zehar pee chuka hoon, tumhi ne mujhe diya, ab tum to zindagi ki duayein mujhe na do.

Chhote baagiche neeche wale ghar ke lounge mein full volume par TV chala raha tha. Nudrat kababo ki tikiyan bana rahi thi, badi dish mein rakh rahi thi. Saath hi sofa par pair rakhe Haneen mobile number mila rahi thi. Baar baar call milati, phir kaat deti. Bil-Aakhir ab himmat kar hi li.    Dusri taraf se koi baat karta raha. Phir Nudrat ne usay kehte suna.  "Kya main Alicia se baat kar sakti hoon?"  Woh sar utha kar usay dekhne lagi.

"Main Haneen hoon Hannah!... Pakistan se." Woh zara hichkicha kar keh rahi thi.

"Alicia meri mails ka jawab nahi de rahi. Woh kidhar hai? Darasal mein usko kisi ka paighaam dena tha."  Dusri taraf ki baat woh bohot dhyan se sun rahi thi. Bilkul chup. Phir bagair kuch kahe phone rakh diya.

"Kya hua?" Magar Hannah ne nahi suna. Chup bethi rahi.

Saadi andar aaya, "Salam," kehkar maa ke qareeb sofa pe gir sa gaya. Thaka hua lag raha tha.

"Faris se mile?" Woh umeed se poochhne lagi.  "Jee, aur Phupho se bhi." Woh door khala mein dekhta apni soch mein gum tha.

"Kya woh ab bhi tumhari baat sunne ko tayar nahi?"    "Unka kasoor nahi hai. Unki jagah koi bhi hota toh yahi karta."

"Tumse bhi wahi rawayya hai?"

"Chhodiye, Ammi." Woh chehre par bashashat wapas laate seedha hua aur  haath badha kar chane ki daal aur gosht ke liye mixture ko teen ungliyon mein uthana chaha. Unhone uske haath par chapet raseed ki.

"Hazaar dafa kaha hai, mat khaya karo darmiyan mein. Be-barkati hoti hai."  Magar Nudrat ki dheeth aulaad ko farq nahi padta tha. Saadi ne mixture munh mein rakha aur chabate hue phir se peeche hokar baith gaya. Haneen badastoor sar jhukaye baithi thi. Dafatan unko khayal aaya.

"Saadi, beta wo markaz ke front pe jo bakery hai na, woh log jagah khaali kar rahe hain. Kyun na hum usko kiraye pe le kar koi kaam shuru kar dein?"

"Aapne abhi toh school ki job khatam ki hai. Aur aapki sehat bhi itni achi nahi. Kyun khud ko halkan karti hain?"    "Kharche bohot hain aur tumhari tankhwa se woh poore nahi hote. Main aaj kal yahi soch rahi hoon. Bakery ki jagah kaafi badi hai. Kapdon ka boutique shuru karne ke baare mein kya khayal hai? Agar farigh baithi rahi toh zyada beemaar ho jaungi."

Saadi ne ek nazar unke haathon ko dekha jo mahaarat se kabab ko shakl de rahe the. Kuch yaad kar ke woh muskuraya.  "Aap restaurant khol lein, Ammi. Kisi ko khana khilane se pyaara ehsaan kya hoga bhala?"

"Restaurant?" Woh soch mein uljhi.  "Magar pehle kisi se mashwara kar lijiye ga."

"Kis se karun?"

"Koi bhi kaam shuru karne se pehle do logon se mashwara lete hain, Ammi. Ek woh jisne is kaam mein faida uthaya ho aur ek jisne isme nuksan uthaya ho."  Phir usne Hannah ka chehra dekha jo abhi tak sal baithi thi.

"Katto Begum, restaurant banne se tumhare toh din phir jayenge." Saadi ne usse aawaz di. Usne safed padta chehra uthaya.

"Hashim bhai se baat ho toh unhe bata dijiye ga ke ab Alicia ko unke paiso ki zaroorat nahi rahi."

Kache kabab ka tukda uske halq mein atak gaya. Woh chonka. "Kyun? Kya hua?"

"Usko jab paise chahiye the, tab unhone nahi diye. Phir usne khud hi haasil karne chaha. Wo shock ke aalam me bol rahi thi. Usne kuch doston ke saath mil kar chori karne ki koshish ki. Woh computers mein achi thi, aur kismat mein buri. Sab giraftar ho gaye. Ab woh jail mein hai, ek lambe arse ke liye."

Woh be-yaqeen thi, bilkul haq-baq. Phir ek dum uth kar andar chali gayi. Saadi ab tak saakit wa jaaye baitha tha. Nudrat afsos se kuch keh rahi thi, magar woh nahi sun raha tha. Aur phir jab shock utra, toh har taraf taasuf chha gaya.

Inhi patharon par chal kar agar aa sako toh aao, Mere ghar ke raste mein koi kahkashan nahi hai.

Qasr Kardar mein mulazimo ki chahal-pahal jaari thi. Sarma ki woh dhund aamiz subah baahar tak mehdood thi. Andar central heating ne lounge ko garam rakha tha. Nayi ladki Fiona ek indoor paudhe ko paani de rahi thi. Gahe-bagahe nigah utha kar Aurangzeb ke kamre ki simt bhi dekh leti, jahan darwaza adha khula tha aur woh aaine ke samne khade tayar ho rahe the. Fiona wahan se mukammal manzar nahi dekh sakti thi, awaaz bhi madham thi. Magar jhagde ki awaaz behra bhi samajh sakta hai, woh toh sirf zuban se na aashna thi. Agar andar jhaako toh samne couch pe taang pe taang jamaye Jawahirat baithi thi. Sulagti aankhein Aurangzeb ki pusht par jami thi.

"Agar tum ek dafa Shero ki baat sun kar..."

"Apne bete ki sifarish mat karo mere samne. Main uski shakal bhi nahi dekhna chahta." Woh talkhi se kehte tie ki knot baandh rahe the.

"Woh kitna hyper hai tum jaante ho. Is tarah ka rawayya rakhoge toh woh ghar chhod kar chala jayega."

"Toh chala jaaye. Do din footpath par rehna padega toh aqal aa jayegi. Apne baap ko bewakoof banata hai."

"Agar woh gaya, toh Aurangzeb, toh iske zimmedar tum hoge." Woh mushkil se apna gussa rok kar boli thi.

"Har cheez ki zimmedar tum ho. Tumhari be-ja himayat ne usko is maqam par la khada kiya hai." Collar jhatak kar coat pehna, ek thandi nazar aaine mein peeche nazar aati Jawahirat pe daali, aur phir baahar nikal gaye. Woh wahi baithi, kansti reh gayi.

Lounge mein woh lamha bhar ko ruke. Nausherwan seedhiyon ke beech mein khada tha. Khamosh, fikarmand sa. Aurangzeb ne uspe nazar daali aur itni jaldi hatayi jaise koi nagawar manzar saamne ho. Mary ko aawaaz di, aur wapas kamre mein chale gaye. Fiona jaldi se paani rakh kar Mary ko bulane bhaagi.

Nausherwan zine pe baitha. Gardan jhuka li. Na paise haath mein rahe, na rishte. "Kitne din tak yunhi baithe rahoge?"
