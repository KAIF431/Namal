Mai Tumhein Sirf Ek Goli Maarunga Zamar. I'm Sorry. Usne un takleef da yaadon ko zehan se jhatkna chaaha magar yeh aasan na tha.

"So?" Dono aamne samne baithay thay aur woh uski aankhon mein dekhte hue muntazir tha.

"Main Tumhein Sunne Aayi Hoon. Tum Dhai Saal Se Yehhi Darkhwast Karte Rahe Ho. To Ab Main Yahan Hoon. Kaho Jo Bhi Kehna Hai."

Faris ke labo pe talakh muskurahat bikhar gayi. "Der Ki Aap Ne Aane Mein. Ab Mujhe Aap Ke Qanoon Se Koi Umeed Nahi Rahi."

Woh khamoshi se use dekhti gayi. "Bataoo Kya Kehna Hai Mujhe Aap Se?"

Usne haath baaham mila kar mez par rakhe aage ko jhuka aur chaba chaba kar har lafz ada kiya. "Yehi Ke Meri Biwi Ki Maut Ki Zimmedar Aap Bhi Hain. Aap Ko Chahiye Tha Ke Aap Uska Haath Pakadti Aur Wahan Se Bhaag Jaati. Aap Ko Usse Bachana Chahiye Tha. Uski Hifazat Karni Chahiye Thi. Magar Apni Dusron Ko Qail Karne Ki Maharat Par Yakeen Kar Ke Aap Ne Usse Bhi Nuqsan Pahunchaya Aur Khud Ko Bhi."

Zumar apni karshi ke hath pe rakhtey ungli thodi tale jamey use dekh rahi thi. "Mere Paas Apne Defend Mein Kehne Ko Koi Lambi Chawdi Baat Nahi Hai. Dil Uchaat Ho Gaya Hai Is Qanoon Se. Sirf Itna Kahunga Ke Teen Saal Aap Ke Sheher Mein Guzarey Itna To Jaanti Thi Aap Mujhe Ke Ek Dafa Meri Baat Sun Leti. Itna To Yaad rakhti Ke Aap Meri Teacher Thi. Ek Dafa To Tasveer Ka Doosra Rukh Dekhti."

Woh ruk gaya ke shayad woh kuch bole magar woh chup chaap sun rahi thi. Naak ki long hanooz damak rahi thi. Faris ne us long pe nazar jamaayi to lehjay ki karwahat zail hone lagi. Aisab qadre dheele parray.

"Mujhe Qatil Samajhti Hain To Samajhiye Madam! Jo Dil Mein Aaye Samajhiye, Magar Ek Dafa Mere Case Ko Zaroor dekhiye Aur Woh Bhi Khud dekhiye." Woh wapas peeche ho gaya.

"Kuch Kehengi Nahi Aap?" Ab ke uska lehja dheema tha. Narm tha.

"Mai kehne nahi aayi thi. Sunne aayi thi, Kyunke agar kehne pe aayi to awaaz bahar tak jaayegi." Woh gehri saans lete hue, thande andaaz mein uth khadi hui. Sapat nazron se uski aankhon ko dekha.

"Yaqinan tum keh chuke ho jo kehna tha, so mulaqat khatam hui." Aur kursi dhakel kar darwaze ki taraf.

Faris ne behad takleef se use jaate dekha aur phir aankhein michh kar gardan jhuka di. Jab woh wapas aaya to Ahmer saahen ke us kone mein muntazir sa tahel raha tha. Use aate dekh kar bechaini se lapka.

"Kya keh rahi thi chudail?" Umeed aur khushi se usne poocha.

"Woh apne zameer ko mutmain karne aayi thi aur na usse ab bhi yaqeen hai ke main mujrim hoon." Ahmer ki aankhon mein uljhan ubhri. "Magar kaha kya usne?"

"Kuch nahi, kyunke agar woh kuch kahegi to awaazain bahar tak jaayengi." Woh deewar se kamar laga kar khada ho gaya. Andaaz dheela dhala sa tha."Lekin woh aayi to sahi na. Aahista aahista hi insaan pighalta hai."

"Woh phir nahi aayegi Stepney. Mujhe ek mauqa mila aur maine woh bhi ganwa diya. Usse qail nahi kar saka main." Woh gardan mod kar aankhein salaam se dhoop ki simt dekhne laga. Umeed ki kirne ab sooraj se bhi nikalna band ho gayi thi.

"Lekin chudail ko chahiye tha ke—"

"Agar tumne ek dafa phir usko chudail kaha to main apna haath tumhare jabre tak le jaane par majboor ho jaunga aur iske nateeje mein tum in do teen daant ganwa doge." Woh jitne taham'mul se bola tha, Ahmer chalti zubaan us tezi se band hui.

Phir "huh" keh kar sir jhatka.

Shafe andaaz-e-bayaan rang badal deta hai!! Warna duniya mein koi baat nayi baat nahi.

Zumar ghar mein daakhil hui to lounge se awaaz aa rahi thi. Haneen aayi hui thi. Woh usi taraf aa gayi. Bade Abba wheel chair pe baithe muskara kar usse dekh rahe the aur Hannah sofa pe pair ooper kar ke baithi, unko kisi Korean drama ki kahani suna rahi thi. Khoob maze se, muskara muskara kar, aankhein ghuma ghuma kar.

Zumar ko chaukhat mein dekh kar uski bolti band ho gayi. Sanjeeda ho kar pair utaare. Aahista se salaam kiya. Abba ne mud kar dekha. Woh thaki thaki si samne sofa par aa baithi.

"Tumhe der ho gayi aaj?" Unhone poocha. Haneen sir jhuka kar apne haathon ko dekhne lagi.

"Main court se seedha jail chali gayi thi. Faris se milne."

Haneen ne current kha kar sir uthaya. Woh sarsari sa bata kar, Sadaqat ko awaaz dene lagi ke uski cheezein le jaaye. "Faris se? Kya baat hui?" Abba ke be-yaqeen alfaaz atke.

"Woh chahta tha main usko sunoon, maine sun liya." Sadaqat andar aaya to woh use cheezein thamane lagi. Chanin jaldi se aage hui, saari narazgi bhula kar tezi se poocha. "Aur kya maan bhi liya?"

"Usne kaha ke woh be-gunah hai, bas. Aur jail mein koi aisa shakhs maqid nahi jo yeh fiqra mantar ki tarah na dohraata ho." Woh thakaan se kanpati masal rahi thi.

"Phupho, main unke saath thi, maine police ko bhi bataya tha, woh be-gunah hain." Woh tadap kar boli thi. Zumar ne aankhein khol kar use dekha, ungli se barabar kanpati maslti rahi.

"Hannah, bacche, main tumhein kathere mein nahi khada karna chahti."

"Magar aap ek dafa mujhse to puchhti ke kya hua tha?"

"Okay, Haneen Yousuf!" Usne sar asbat me hilaya, peeche ho kar baithi, taang par taang jamayi. "Shuru karte hai phir,"

Hannah ne kamar seedhi kar li. Bade Abba khamoshi se bebasi mein un dono ko dekhne lage jo aapas mein aamne samne baithi thi lekin Dono ke darmiyan bohot sa faasla tha.

"Us roz, jab mujh pe firing ki gayi, tum hotel ke kamre mein thi, ek se saare teen baje takreeban?"

"Ji!" Usne gardan hilaayi.

"Aur is dauraan Faris kahin nahi gaya?" Zumar sanjeedgi se pooch rahi thi.

"Nahi, woh hamare saath the."

"Aur is dauraan tum bhi kahin uth kar nahi gayi?" "Ji nahi."

"Tum saara waqt usi kamre mein thi?"

"Jii"

"Aur is dauraan tumne Faris se nazar nahi hatayi? Faris aur Alicia ke siwa kisi se koi baat bhi nahi ki?"

"Nahi."

"Police ko bhi tumne bilkul yehi kaha tha. Kya main ise tumhara hatmi bayan tasavvur kar loon?"

"Jii. Ma'am procescutor!" kaafi aitmaad se gardan akdaye wo boli. Zumar ne aankhein michhi, gehri saans li, aur uth kar baahar nikal gayi. Chand lamhon baad, jab wapas aayi, to uske haath mein wahi box tha jo woh almari mein jooton ke khane mein rakhti thi.

"Ye tumhari Ammi ke mobile ka bill hai. Woh mobile jo us roz tumhare paas tha."Haneen ne qadar-e-hairat se woh kaaghaz thama, aur jab uspe nigah daali, to uska chehra safed padne laga.

"Tumne police ko bhi kaha ke tumne is dauraan kisi se koi baat nahi ki, yaani ek lamhe ko bhi tum Faris se ghafil nahi thi. Jab ke is bill ke mutabiq tumne dedh baje apne ghar char minute, aur paune teen baje apni ek dost ko dus minute ke liye call ki." Phir ek doosra kaaghaz uske samne kiya.

"Yeh us hotel ki lobby ke CCTV camera ka ek still image hai. Isme tum neeche ek shop mein khadi dikhai de rahi ho, aur waqt hua hai 02:17. Magar tumne kaha tha ke tum is dauraan kamre se kahin nahi gayi?"

"Main batana bhool gayi thi, aur... aur yeh firing se bohot pehle ka waqt hai." Usne bujhe chehre ke saath wazaahat dene ki koshish ki.

"Hannah, maine tumse is baare mein koi baat is liye nahi ki kyunki main jaanti thi, tum dhai ghante ek kamre mein tik kar nahi baith sakti. Main yeh bhi jaanti hoon ke tumne yeh baatein kyun nahi batayi, tum usko protect karna chahti thi. Magar Hannah, yeh goli ka maamla hai, aur gawaahi ke maamle mein humein agar kisi ki ek baat jhoot maaloom ho to uski baaki saari baatein bhi sachhi nahi rehti."

"Main thak gayi hoon... Aram karne ja rahi hoon. Aap log baatein karein." Woh narmi se kehti, kaaghzaat wapas dabbe mein daalti uth gayi. Haneen chehra jhukaye kitni hi der usi tarah baithi rahi, aur Abba bas afsos se use dekhte rahe. Agar unke khandan ke saare log ek din ke liye apni zahanat pe bharosa karna chhod de to kitna acha ho.
