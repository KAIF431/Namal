Chapter 24

⏱️ 6 Min Read • 1,052 Words • Author: Nimra Ahmed

"Main copy kar deta hoon flash drive mein. Bhai office nahi gaye to laptop ghar pe rakh ke gaye honge."Woh chalta hua saath wale kamray mein aaya. Batti on ki.

"Jaldi karna, main is kamray mein zyada der nahi rukna chahti," usne flash drive badhate hue kaha.Noshervan ne drive pakadte hue nazar bhar ke uske chehre ko dekha. "Main samajh sakta hoon," woh jawaban zakhmi sa muskaraaya.

Noshervan ne Hashim ki study table se laptop uthaya aur on kiya. Shehrain uske saath khadi ho kar dekhne lagi. Saath hi woh lab kaat rahi thi aur ungliyaan bhi maror rahi thi.

"Woh... password? Ab yeh kya hai?" Sab kuch theek hote hue jab password manga gaya to Noshervan karaah utha.Shehrain ke maathe par bal pad gaye.

"Maine kaha tha na tumse nahi hoga... jaane do," woh mudne lagi.

"Ek minute thehro toh!" Usne mobile nikaal kar Hashim ko call milai.

"Mera naam le lena, taa ke woh bilkul bhi apna password na de," woh mitti se boli. Noshervan ne khamosh rehne ka ishara kiya. Woh behtar aur samajhdar nazar aane ki koshish kar raha tha.

"Haan Shero, bolo." Woh masroof tha.

"Bhai yaar, aapke laptop ka password kya hai?"

"Kyoon? Kya hua?" Apni tamam tar masroofiyat ke bawajood woh chonka tha.

"Kuch pictures chahiyein thi Sonia ke liye."

"Kaun si pictures?" Woh Hashim tha—khatak gaya.

"Bhai de rahe ho ya mai kuch aur karoon?" Uska mood bigadne laga.

"Phir... hmm... acha." Keh kar sir hila kar phone band kiya aur muskaraya. Keyboard ke button dabaye.

Uske kandhay se jhankti Shehrain ne unko hifz kiya (go ke iski zarurat na thi) aur phir la-parwahi se udhar dekhne lagi. (Yeh lafz to usko az bar tha. Aankhein band karke bhi type kar sakti thi.)

"Aap batati jaayein, kaun kaun si chahiyein?" Unki honeymoon, shaadi aur deegar moqay ki tasaveer khulti ja rahi thi. Maqsad poora hone ke baad Shehrain ko jaane ki jaldi thi, lekin woh dekh kar seene mein kuch chhanay laga tha — ehsas-e-zayaan, tehi damani.

"Yeh wali... aur yeh teenon..." Woh ungli se screen pe ishara karti bataane lagi.Noshervan ne copy karte hue uske chehre ko dekha. Woh zabt karti hui nazar aa rahi thi. Usne afsos, hamdardi aur tarahum sab mehsoos kiya tha — siwaye qareeb ki boo ke.

———

"Main to lab khol ke pa-band-e-salaasil thehraTeri aur baat hai, tu sahib-e-mahfil thehra"

Kamra-e-imtihaan mein mamool ka sanata chhaya tha. Do mutanazim khawateen kursiyon ki qataaron ke beech tahal rahi thein. Ladkiyaan sar jhukaaye likhay ja rahi thi. Haneen ne dafa'tan dard karti ungliyon ko sehlatay huey sar uthaya aur phir gardan ko relax karte hue daayein taraf dekha. Kamray ki ek deewar khidki se dhaki thi, aur saamne sarrak aur banglon ki qataar nazar aa rahi thi.

Jis law college ko unka imtihaani markaz banaya gaya tha, woh darasal aik bara sa bangla tha. Aur yeh kamra yaqeenan drawing-dining ke tor par istemal ke liye banaya gaya hoga — usne socha. Neeche lawn tha aur wahan se un adheer umar wakeel sahab ki car nikalti dikhai de rahi thi jo High Court ke wakeel thay, is law college ke malik thay aur har paper mein baar baar imtihaani kamron ka chakkar laga kar apni kharab angrezi mein ladkiyon ko naqal karne ke nataij se daranay ki koshish karte thay.

Shukar ke ab woh nahi ja rahe thay aur aglay derh ghantay sar par sawar nahi honge — usne muskara ke socha aur dobara parche par jhuk gayi.

"Ishhh!" Naeema ne peechay se usse hila diya.

Usne jhunjhla ke mumtahin ko dekha jinki taraf unki peeth thi, aur phir peechay murhi."Kya hai?""Rafia ko do," usne tissue aagay kiya.

Haneen ne jaldi se tissue pakra jaise koi jalta hua angara ho aur Rafia ki kamar mein chhaan chhaan chubha kar use mutawajjah kiya.

Mumtahin ab chalti hui aagay ja rahi thi. Qataar khatam kar ke hi woh mur'ti, aur is se pehle hi usne Rafia ko woh de dena tha.

Magar Rafia ya to darr gayi thi, ya usse samajhne mein ghalti hui, ya mumtahin ghalat waqt pe mur gayi. Usse hila kar tissue pakraati Haneen ke haath se tissue gir gaya.

Woh foran paper pe jhuki. Uski ghabrahat ne sab wazeh kar diya. Mumtahin khatoon tez tez us taraf aayi. Jhuk kar tissue uthaya. Usay khola.

Haneen ne sar jhukaye agla lafz likhne ki koshish ki magar haath nam ho gaye, parcha jahanum ho gaya — siyahi phelne lagi.

"Aap naqal istemal kar rahi thein? Kahan se aaya yeh aap ke paas? Chhoro, paper do!"

Haathon ne uska parcha kheench liya. Do teachers mazeed us taraf aayi. Woh haka baka si baithi reh gayi.

"Yeh mera nahi hai, ma'am! Mujhe nahi pata is mein kya hai!""Jhoot mat bolo. Main ne khud tumhein ise pakray dekha hai!"

"Yeh Naeema ne diya tha — Rafia ko dene ke liye," usne pichhli aur agli dono ko ghasita — ke woh koi uski achi dostein na thein jinhein woh bacha leti.

"Mera naam kyun le rahi ho?""Mujhe nahi pata yeh kya keh rahi hai!" — dono la-ta'alluq ho gayi.

Kamray mein tamasha lag gaya. Sab sar utha ke dekhne lage. Teachers usay utha rahi thein ke woh apni cheezein le kar office mein aa jaye.

Uska paper khatam."Aap par case banay ga aur thaanay mein darj ho ga. Teen saal tak aap paper nahi de sakteen."

Un ke alfaaz Haneen Yousuf ki rooh qabz kar rahe thay.

Zameen o aasman uski nigahon ke samne ghoomne lage. Aaj to waise bhi aakhri paper tha. Yeh aik dum se sab kaise ghalat hone lag gaya tha?

Kuch ladkiyan wapas likhne mein masroof ho gayi, kuch usay cheezein samet'te dekh rahi thi."Ma'am, yeh mera nahi hai... mujhe nahi pata tha is mein kya likha hai..."Woh khushk halaq ke saath keh rahi thi.

Kisi ne usay tissue pass karte nahi dekha tha. Superintendent ne nashta us ke paas dekha tha, aur agli-pichhli unhein dumb loma loomri ka shikaar lagi thi.

Sirf usay uthaya gaya. Woh minnat karti rahi, kabhi gusse se zor se bhi boli, magar koi asar nahi.

Madam use do kamron se guzaar kar aik office numa kamray mein le aayi. Usay kursi pe bitha diya. Parcha paperweight ke neeche rakh diya. Aur ek doosri teacher ko university ki inspection team ko call karne ka keh diya.
