CHAPTER 45

Chapter 45

⏱️ 7 Min Read • 1,224 Words • Author: Nimra Ahmed

Darwaza khula. Usne andar qadam rakha. Bina dekhe deewar pe haath maara aur seedha doosra button dabaya. Dakhli hissa roshan ho gaya. Woh chhote chhote qadam uthata hua andar aaya. Gardan ghuma kar chhat, khidkiyon, deewaron ko dekhta hua woh rahdaari se guzar raha tha. Ghar baahir se paint shuda tha, ke Kardars apna ghar paint karwate to iska bhi baahir ka hissa karwa dete, kyunke unke lawn se yeh dikhai deta tha. Albata andar se ghar mamooli tha. Normal furniture, chipboard ka farsh, deewaron aur chhat ke milne ki jagah pe ukhda paint.

Woh aage barhta gaya. Jawahiraat ne yahaan ki safai karwa di thi. Aaj ghar saaf suthra tha, magar phir bhi purana aur mamooli lagta tha. Lounge chhota sa tha. Uske ek taraf khaane ki gol table rakhi thi. Drawing room alag tha. Seedhiyaan upar ja rahi thi. Ek taraf darwaza tha jahan se basement ko jaane wali seedhiyaan thi. Basement tehkhana tha — poore ghar ke raqbe pe phaila hua ek kamra jismein satoon the lekin deewarein nahi thi.

Is tehkhaane mein kuch ajeeb tha. Faris udhar nahi gaya. Woh upar ke manzil pe aaya. Aage terrace bhi tha aur andar ek deewar par ek tasveer thi. Tasveer mein woh halka sa muskara raha tha — bilkul halka sa. Ash grey dinner suit mein tha, baal waisay hi jaise aaj kal hote. Saath mein ek ladki thi, saari mein libaas mein, step mein kiye gaye baal, bade jhumkay, jaazib nazar. Woh bhi muskura rahi thi.

Faris palat gaya. Uska chehra be-ta’sur tha. Haath dho kar woh room mein aaya, sleeves moad kar wuzu karne laga.

Terrace se baahir roshni mein nahaaya hua qasr dikhayi de raha tha. Andar mulazimon ki chahal-pahal jaari thi. Jawahiraat sarbarahi kursi par baitha, nazaakat se chhuri-kaanta chala kar steak ka tukda kaat rahi thi. Dayen haath baitha Hashim plate pe jhuka khaane mein masroof tha. Uske mobile ka message tone waqfa waqfa se baj raha tha. Jawahiraat ke doosre haath baitha Nosherwan be-dili se kaanta plate mein idhar udhar ghoomaa raha tha. Uski shave barhi hui thi.

“Tum aaj phir office nahi aaye.” Jawahiraat ne kaanta chalate bas nigaahein utha kar Shero ko dekha. Usne bezaari se chehra uthaya.

“Aap log mujhe kuch der ke liye akela nahi chhod sakte?”

“Mee…” Hashim ne nigaahon mein Jawahiraat ko tanbeeh ki. Usne zara se shaane uchkaaye.

“Mera khayal tha tum ab tak apne bhai ko samjha chuke hoge. Magar yeh hanuuz us aurat ke gham mein hai jo use gadha samajh kar istemaal kar ke chali gayi.”

“Aap chahti hain mein table se uth jaaun?” Uska chehra surkh padne laga.

“Shero, badtameezi mat karo. Woh hamari maa hain.” Aur jis tarah Hashim ne sirf nigaahein utha kar khecha tha, Nosherwan ne gardan jhuka li.

Jawahiraat ne gehri saans lekar glass honton se lagaya.“Mein us din ka intezaar kar rahi hoon jab tumhein ehsaas hoga ke tumhari maa aur tumhara bhai tumhein protect karne ke liye kya kya karte hain. Aur yeh poora hafta humne tumhara khamakha ka gussa bardasht kiya hai. Tum humein hi mored-e-ilzam thehra rahe ho? Agar Saadi ne (aur is naam par Nosherwan ki nasain phatne ko thi) kuch bura kiya bhi hai to tumhare bhai ke saath. Aur jab woh keh raha hai ke woh use sambhal lega to tum kyun apna khoon jala rahe ho?”

Nosherwan ne kaanta rakh diya. Bas kha chuka tha woh.

“Faris chala gaya?” Hashim ne jaan bujh kar maa ko dekhte hue mauzu badla. Woh abhi thande andaaz mein Shero ki mazeed class le sakti thi, magar…

Hashim ke musalsal nigaahein utha utha kar tanbeeh dene pe, Jawahiraat ne gehri saans le kar bola, “Mehmaan se chaar din baad badboo aane lagti hai. So aaj uska ghar tayar karwa diya tha.”

Nosherwan uthne ke liye par tol raha tha magar har haal mein us mein itni jurat na thi ke bade bhai aur maa ke saamne se yun uth jaaye.

Hashim ka mobile phir baja. Usne ek haath se kaanta honton tak le jaate hue doosre se phone kaan se lagaya.“Ji ji, aap ka kaam ho gaya tha. Mein subah tak case file aap ko bhijwa doonga. Ji bilkul.”Usne plate paray ki aur doosra number milane laga. Hashim ke har waqt bajte phone ke woh aadhi thay.

“Ji, Zumar kaisee hain aap?”Un dono ne chonk kar use phone pe kehte suna.“Mein ne aap ko ek case file ka kaha tha. Okay. Woh copy ho gayi? Achha. Mein driver ko bhej deta hoon. Aap ke ghar se pick kar le ga.”Usne ruk kar suna.

“Aap kidhar hain? Khairiyat? Saadi ki taraf? Achha.” Hashim baat dohraane ka aadhi na tha, magar kyunke yeh uske liye bhi ghair mutawaqqa tha so woh dohrata gaya. Nigaahein utha kar Shero ko dekha. Woh bhonwain bheenche usi ko dekh raha tha.

“Chalein, jab aap wapas aayein. Achha. Subah wahin se court jaayein gi? Okay. Koi masla nahi. Aa… Saadi kareeb hai to meri baat karwa dein.”Woh kehte hue apne chhote bhai ko dekh raha tha. Jawahiraat bhi napkin se lab thapthapati udhar hi mutawajjeh thi.

“Kya haal hai Saadi?” Woh bola to aankhon mein sard mehri dar aayi. Nosherwan ne “huhn” keh kar istehzaaiya sar jhatka.

“Mein to bilkul theek hoon. Aisa hai ke subah meri secretary tumhein call kar ke kal ki appointment degi. Zaroor aana. Mein intezaar karoonga.”Keh kar usne phone rakh diya.

“Yeh kya? Tum ne use DA ki nazron se giraya ke woh ek dafa phir family ban gaye? Woh kal aaye ga. Mein us se baat karoonga. Aur mein sab sambhaal loonga. Ab waqt aa gaya hai ke tum Saadi Yusuf obsession (asaib) se nikal aao.”Har fikra tod tod kar tahammul se ada kiya.

“Nosherwan, relax.”Jawahiraat ne ab ke narmi se Shero ka haath thaama. Usne aapparent khud ko normal karte hue asbaat mein sar hila diya.Baharhaal ta’suraat chhupane mein maa aur bhai jaisa maahir na tha.

“Yeh koi itni badi baat nahi hai. Badi baat tab hoti agar Saadi ke haath kuch aisa lagta jo humein nuqsan de sakta.”

“Mein samajh gaya. Mein theek hoon.”Woh apna mobile nikaalte hue uth gaya.

Jawahiraat ne qadray tashweesh se gardan utha kar usay dekha.“Kahan jaa rahe ho?”“Sarmad waghera ne baahir khaane ka program banaya tha. Pehle inkaar kar diya, ab chala hi jaata hoon. Mood achha ho jaaye ga. Warna jab tak yeh Saadi Yusuf zinda hai meri zindagi masail ka shikaar hi rahe gi.”Sar jhatak kar kehta woh nikalne laga. Phir jaise apni hi baat ne soch ka ek naya darwaza khola.“Mar kyun nahi jaata yeh Saadi aakhir? Itne to bomb blast hote hain roz!”

Woh to keh kar nikal gaya magar Hashim be ikhtiyaar saans roke usko dekhne laga.“Soch samajh kar bola karo!”Usne aqab se qadray barhami se pukara. Shero ne mure baghair “bye” ka haath hilaaya aur aage barhta gaya.

“Mujhe yaqeen nahi ke woh doston ke paas jaa raha hai.”“Agar aap isi tarah har waqt usay manfi rukh dikhati rahin to woh waqai kisi ke paas jaane ke qabil nahi rahe ga.”

“Tumhare khayal mein mein uski bhalai nahi chahti?”“Kya hum sukoon se khaana kha sakte hain?”Hashim wapas plate ki taraf mutawajjeh hua.

“Sure!” Jawahiraat ne nazaakat se shaane uchkaaye. Ungli se samne gire baal peeche kiye aur ghoont ghoont juice peene lagi.
