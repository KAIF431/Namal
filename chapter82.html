"Kya aap ki ammi... Aap se mera zikar kiya tha pichle hafte?"

Zara teher kar bola. Zumar ne chonk kar usay dekha. Phir na samjhi se nafi mein gardan hilayi.  "Nahi, kyun?"

Aur Faris bas usay dekh kar reh gaya. Phir halka sa, nafi mein sar hilaaya.

"Yunhi. Aap ke abu se milna tha to. Mera khayal hai woh mujhe pasand nahi karti, Khair jaane de. Apna khayal rakhiyega."
