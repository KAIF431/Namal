CHAPTER 48

Chapter 48

⏱️ 9 Min Read • 1,461 Words • Author: Nimra Ahmed

Achanak se dil palat gaya to B.A. mein daakhila le liya. Dil to kabhi bhi palat jaata hai na phuppo!Uska naakhoon akhbaar ka kinaara ragarta naakhun mazeed tez ho gaya. Sar jhuka kar woh baray Abba ko koi doosri khabar sunaane lagi. Albata ab ke andaaz sust tha.Zumar ne jaate jaate mudh kar usay dekha. Yeh aakhri fiqra kehtay uski awaaz mein na tarz tha na thehrai, bas ajeeb si udaasi thi. Woh raahdaari se guzarti Saadi ke kamray ke darwaazay tak aayi to woh aainay ke samne khara nazar aaraha tha. Collar kare hue uparkhare thay aur woh tie ki girah laga raha tha. Zumar zara sa muskurayi. Darwaaza haule se bajaya.“To tumhara koi office bhi hai?”Girah kheench kar ooper le jaate woh jungi se palta aur collar durust kiye.“Do saal mein pehli dafa chhutti li hai, woh bhi sirf do haftay ki. Aur boss se chaprasi tak har banda guzarte guzarte taane de jaata hai.Aap to aise mat karein.”“Oh! Aur itni lambi chhutti kyun li?”Saadi chup ho gaya. (Judge pe aakhri dino mein pressure daala tha, Mamoon ko khilwana tha, Hashim bhai ka laptop hack karta tha, jiska mauqa aap ke zariye mil hi gaya, aur ab in files ko kholna hai magar chhutti khatam...)Yeh sab sirf socha. Jab bola to bas itna, “Kuch research work kar raha tha, usko mukammal karna tha.”“Chalo phir weekend pe milne ka plan karte hain.”“Ji. Aap to shaadi mein nahi aayengi na?” Usne sarsari saaz chheda. Woh jo matarne lagi thi, chonk gayi.“Kis ki shaadi?”“Ab poora rishta maloom nahi, magar jis ladke ki shaadi hai woh hamara bhi rishtedaar hai aur us Hammad ka bhi. Hammad aur Kiran is liye to aaye hue hain Australia se. Woh bhi honge shaadi pe. Aur Kiran Kardar khandaan ko bil-khusoos bulwaye gi. Woh sab bhi honge. So udhar aap Hammad ka saamna nahi kar sakengi mujhe pata hai. Is liye aap ka card udhar aaya to main ne Ammi se kaha ke phuppo ko na bhejein. Woh nahi aayengi.”Zumar ke lab bheench gaye aur aankhon ki patliyaan sukad gayin. Seene pe baazu lapait kar usay tundi se dekha.“Aur tumhein kyun laga ke main uska saamna nahi kar sakti?”“Aap nahi kar sakti. Tabhi to khandaan ki kisi taqreeb pe nahi jaati hain. Khair, aap ne nahi jaana to koi baat nahi. Main samajh sakta hoon.”Bohat samajhdari se usne kaha.“Main is liye nahi jaati kyunke waqt nahi milta aur...”“Weekend pe waqt hoga phir?” Woh tezi se bola.Zumar ne be-dhiyaani se “haan” kaha to usne fori poocha, “Matlab aap chalengi?”“Main dekhoongi.” Woh ruk kar boli. Phir ghari dekhi. Usay ab chalna tha. Woh nikli to Saadi mukammal tayar ho kar nikhar nikhar sa bahar nikla. Lounge mein bas baray Abba thay. Haneen sonay chali gayi thi. Unhon ne usay sawalia nazron se dekha.“Woh taqreeb pe jaane ke liye maan gayi?”

---

“Bilkul!” Muskurakar kehtay usne chai ka cup uthaya aur samne baith gaya. Baray Abba ne taajjub se usay dekha.“Tum ne kaise raazi kiya usay? Main kehta to kabhi na maanti.”“Ab aap ke paas Saadi Yusuf jaisa dimaag thori hai.”Ghoont bharte woh muskuraya. Phir kitchen ki taraf rukh karke awaaz lagayi,“Ammi! Aap nashta Lahore se la rahi hain ya kitchen se?”“Kitchen se. Main ne jootha phainkna hai tumhare qad ka lihaaz kiye baghair.”Woh tray uthaye masnooi khafgi se bolti aa rahi thin.Saadi ne dada ko dekha.“Koi maane ga ke yeh khatoon mere peechay mere behan bhai ko meri misaalein deti hain?”“Mujhe pata hai achhi tarah. Jaldi jaldi ka shor is liye machate ho ke nashta aadha karna paday. Ab agar tum ne yeh khatam na kiya na Saadi, to mujhe Ammi na kehna.”Woh samne baithtay hue uski shikayat dada se laga rahi thin.Woh muskuratay hue bas sun raha tha. Saadi ne hasb-e-‘aadat bas thoda sa khaya. Phir haath saaf karta utha aur bohot mutanat se maan ko mukhatib kiya.“Acha, Nudrat behen! Allah Hafiz.”Aur is se pehle ke woh waqai uske qad ka lihaaz kiye baghair aik haath jor detin, woh bahar nikal chuka tha.

Sarah office ke liye tayar car ka darwaaza khol rahi thi jab gate ki ghanti baji. Usne murh kar dekha. Gate ooncha tha. Yahan se maloom nahi hota tha ke bahar kaun hai. Usne chaabi darwaazay mein chhodi, bag car ki chhat pe rakh kar gate tak aayi aur usay khola.Aadha darwaaza khultay hi haath thithak kar rukay.Bahar Faaris khara tha. T-shirt, jeans, chhote katay baal, sanjeeda gehri nazrein aur supaat chehra.Sarah ne baaqi darwaaza susti se khola.“Faaris?”Koi na-deed walt kan ke peechay adasti woh ek taraf hatti. Chehray pe tazabzub sa darr aaya tha.“Aap theek hain?”Sarsari sa sawal kiya. Albata usko dekh kar gehri nazar se rahi thi.“Hun,” mein sar zara sa hila kar mazeed ek qadam peechay hua.“Mera itni subah aana achha nahi laga? Yaa Aana hi?”Uski hichkichahat ke baais woh zara sard andaaz mein bola.Sarah ke chehray par sharmindagi ubhri.“Aisa nahi hai... aao.”“Bachiyon se milne aaya tha main.”Woh wahin khara raha. Sarah bhi udhar hi khadi rahi magar usse nigah nahi milayi.“Woh school ke liye tayar ho rahi hain. Bas hum nikalne hi walay thay.”Saath hi usne ghari dekhi jaise jaldi mein ho.“Yani kisi aur waqt aaoon?”Uske chehray ke badaltay rang baghoor dekhte woh khushk andaaz mein keh raha tha. Sarah ne iztirab se aankh utha kar usay dekha.“Tum aa saktay ho, Faaris.”“Main zyada nahi aau?”Woh uske ta’assuraat parh raha tha.“Toh aap ke khyaal mein Waris ko main ne qatal kiya tha?”“Aisa nahi hai. Mujhe yaqeen hai tumhein phansaya gaya tha. Yaqeenan tumhare dushman bohot honge aur...”“...Aur mera idhar aana aap ke khandaan ke liye khatray ka baais ban sakta hai. Main samajh gaya.”Sar hila kar woh yun keh raha tha jaise waqai samajh gaya ho. Sarah ne dukh se usay dekha.“Faaris! I’m sorry. Magar main pehle hi bohot mushkil zindagi guzaar rahi hoon. Mere paas meri betiyon ke ilawa koi nahi hai. Main unko kisi bhi khatray mein nahi daal sakti. Tum please mujhe ghalat mat lena.”“...Kaha na samajh gaya. Ab mil loon ya jaaon?”“Nahi, aao please.”Woh ab ke waqai peechay hatti aur andar ki taraf barhi.Woh chand lamhay zabt se usay aagay jaate dekh ta raha, phir sar jhuka gaya.

Har haqeeqat fraib lagti hai, jab koi aitbaar kho baithay.

Study room mein khamoshi pheli thi. Nosherwan bhi usi khamoshi ka hissa bana, labon pe muthi rakhe, table ke us taraf baitha Hashim ko dekh raha tha jobohot inhemaak se file ke safhay ko parh raha tha.Usay aaj office der se jaana tha. Is liye woh raat wale libaas mein tha.“Teesri dafa pooch raha hoon, Saadi kab aayega?”Woh ab bezar hone laga to muqaddas khamoshi ko tora.“Hun?”Hashim ne safha palta. Phir nigah utha kar usay dekha.“Kya uske intezaar mein tum tamaam raat nahi soye?”Usne Sheru ki halki gulaabi aankhon ko dekh kar kaha tha. Sher ka ooper ka saans ooper aur neeche ka neeche reh gaya. Rangat zara pheeki hui.“Soyaa tha, magar bohot der se,”Usne garbarakar kaha.Phir baghoor Hashim ke ta’assuraat dekhe. Woh phir se file mein masroof ho gaya tha.Laakh shaatir sahi, itni jaldi Hashim ko shak nahi ho sakta tha ke woh phir se drugs pe aa gaya hai.Mobile baja.Hashim ne ungli se button dabaaya aur “bolo” kehte hue file ka doosra safha palta.Us ke paas itni fursat bhi na thi ke mobile kaan se lagaata.Uski secretary ki awaaz goonji,“Sir! Main ne Saadi Yusuf ko call ki thi...”Woh ruk gayi. Hashim ne pen se us safhay mein kuch underline kiya.“Haleema, main aglay kitnay minute tumhare bolne ka intezaar karoon ga?”“Sorry sir! Unhon ne kaha ke woh masroof hain. Unko apna schedule dekhna padega. Aaj to naamumkin hai. Agle haftay mein unko dobara call karke poochhoon agar...”Woh ruki magar phir jaldi se boli,“Agar Hashim bhai ko mujhse milne ka itna hi shauq hai to—”Iss se pehle Hashim ne button off kiya aur safhay mein do alfaaz ke gird daaira lagaya.Wakalah—Saara alfaazon ka khel hi to tha.Sheru ke maathay pe bal pad gaye thay.“Attitude dekha aap ne uska? Badtameez insaan... khud ko samajhta kya hai?”Hashim ne thakaawat se sar nafi mein hila kar Sheru ko dekha.“Tum kab bain-al-sutoor baatein parhna seekhoge, Nosherwan?”Woh jo bhara hua aagay ho kar baitha kuch aur bhi kehna chahta tha, hairani se ruk gaya.“Uski is baat ka aur kya matlab?”“Kya tum Saadi ko nahi jaante? Woh badtameezi nahi kar raha, woh mujhse mulaqat ko taal raha hai.”
