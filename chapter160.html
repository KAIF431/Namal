Chapter 160

⏱️ 7 Min Read • 1,172 Words • Author: Nimra Ahmed

Maut se guzarke yeh kaisi zindagi paayi, Shaakh shaakh hota hai waar ka gumaa'n yaaro...

Jawahirat Kardar ke kamre mein heater ki numaaish thi. Dopehar ke waqt band pardo ki wajah se andhera chhaya hua tha. Wo gardan tale phule phule takiye rakhe, siyah reshmi lehaaf mein leti, veeran aur bimaar dikhti thi.

Baal kaano ke peechhe arshe, Aankhon ke neeche halke, royi jaisi aankhon mein.

Makeup ke baghair peela kamzor chehra... wo thi bhi siyaah libaas mein, aur veeraan aankhon se dekh bhi pardo ki siyaahi ko rahi thi. Saadi saamne kursi pe ghutne mila kar baitha, fikar mandi se usse dekh raha tha. Wo uski tabiyat poochhne aaya tha, magar wo soti jaagti kaifiyat mein, bilkul be jaan si dikhayi deti thi, dawaon ka asar shadeed tha.

"Mrs. kardar... Allah aapko akela nahi chhodega. Wo aapko sambhaal lega. Bharosa kar ke dekhein us par, aapka har masla wo hal kar dega." Wo narmi se samjha raha tha jab khidki ko dekhti Jawahirat ke lab phadphadaye.

"Kya tumne wo documentary show dekha hai? Wo ghaarat-gar Predator?"

"Nahi... main darasal..." wo national geographic ya discovery wagera kaha dekhta tha. Waqt hi nahi milta tha.

"Us din uski ek qist lagi. Wo maada (ghaarat-garon) ke baare mein thi. Ghaarat-garon ki malika, maada cheetah, mujhe usne bohot rulaya. Maloom hai kyun?"

"Aap batayein kyun?" Wo narmi se aage ho kar sun ne laga. Wo gardan mode khidki ko dekhti bolti ja rahi thi. Goya uncha sochne ki kaifiyat mein ho.

"Ghaarat-gar jaante ho kya hote hain? Predators, wo janwar jo apne se kamzor ka shikaar karte hain. Tum log samajhte ho, wo bhook mitane ya aadat dohrane ko aisa karte hain, magar nahi... maada cheetah aisi nahi hoti. Kyunki nar cheetah bewafa janwar hai, apni maada ko aulaad ka tohfa de kar chhod jata hai. Maada cheetah apne bachon ko tanha palti hai aur us roz maine dekha, us show mein, ke maada ghaarath-gar hona kitna mushkil hai."

Parday par jami uski aankhein gulaabi padne lagin. Awaaz randehnay lagi. Wo afsos se usse dekhta raha. Use laga, wo apne gham ke baare mein baat nahi karna chahti. Isi liye idhar udhar ki baatein kar rahi hai. Usay yahi laga.

"Wo ek maada cheetah thi aur uske do nanhe bachay the, jinke liye shikaar usi ko dhoondh kar lana tha. Jaante ho, har cheetah ka ek taaqat ka zakheera hota hai, ek shikaar pakadne ke liye wo jitna bhaagta hai, us ke nateeje mein uski taaqat aadhi reh jaati hai. Wo bhi apne bachon ko kuchar mein chhod kar shikaar par nikalti hai, ghaat lagati hai, hiran ke peeche bhaagti hai... oh magar Allah ka nizaam! Hiran jitna bhi bhaag le, taaqat nahi khota, magar wo teer raftaar maada cheetah, hiran ko dabocha bhi leti hai, apni kuchaar mein le bhi aati hai, magar aadhi... uski taaqat khatam ho chuki hoti hai. Nidhhal hai, bachay bhookay hain, magar us se pehle ke wo hiran ke laash ko kha sake, ek babbar sher aa jaata hai. Ek bada ghaarath-gar."

Usne karb se aankhein band ki. Do aansu nikal kar gaalon par ladhak gaye.

"Sher gharjata hai aur wo maada majbooran peeche hat jaati hai. Agar aisa nahi karegi to sher uske dono bachon par jhapat padega aur wo sher ka Muqabla nahi kar sakti. Uske samne sher uska shikaar kha jaata hai aur woh apne panje chaat'ti reh jaati hai."

Sate chehre ke saath woh talakhi se muskaraayi. Woh khamoshi se sunta raha. Us kahani mein koi dilchaspi nahi thi. Sirf Mrs. Kardar ki haalat-e-gham mein daal rahi thi. Hashim ke saath jo bhi masla tha uska, us mein uska toh koi kasoor nahi tha. Woh toh shaayad jaanti bhi na ho ke Hashim ne Waris ko qatl karwaya tha. Aur phir woh toh uski dost rahi thi. Woh uske paas aakar aksar baithta tha, baatein karta tha, uski haalat se woh aur kya mehsoos karta?

"Ab uski aadhi taaqat khatam ho chuki hai. Use kal laazmi shikaar karna hoga, taake woh apni taaqat poori kare, warna woh mar jaayegi aur uske bachay uske baad bhookh se hi mar jaayenge." Woh baat jaari rakhe hue thi. "So agle din woh phir nikalti hai, hiran ke peeche bhaagti hai, usay jaa dabochti hai aur use ghaseet kar ek tanha gose mein le aati hai, apni saari taaqat luta chuki hoti hai, agar yeh hiran bhi koi sher ya bara ghaarat gar le gaya, toh woh mar jaayegi. Aur sabse takleef deh baat, aaj hiran nahi balki hiran ka bacha shikaar kiya hai. Woh itna chhota hai ke use bachon ko de toh apne hissa mein chand luqme hi aayenge, aur woh mar jaayegi. Taqat barabar karne ke liye use yahaan akela hi khana hoga. Toh woh use bachon tak nahi le kar jaati, khud kha leti hai."

Palkein band ki. Aansoo mutawatar gir rahe the.

"Bachay abhi bhi bhooke hain. Agle din woh phir shikaar ke liye daurti hai. Taqat kam hai, kyun ke kal ka hiran chhota tha. So aaj woh ek bada hiran shikaar karti hai. Balakhir ab uske bachay aur woh mil kar use kha sakenge. Woh hiran ka laasha ghaseet kar kuchar tak laati hai, toh.. t-toh" uski awaaz kapkaapaayi. Tap tap girte aansuon mein rawaani aa gayi.

"Toh uske do nanhe cheete wahan nahi thay. Wo jinhe lawaris chhodh kar jati thi. Woh bachay jangli hyenas (lakad bhagon) ke narghe mein hotay hain. Woh qareeb aati hai, hamla nahi karti, jhapatti bhi nahi hai. Sirf gharraati hai aur hyena (lakad bhagga) darr jaati hai. Ma'loom hai kyun? Kyunke maada cheetah ki aankhon tale siyah lines hoti hain, jo gharraate waqt use bohot bar'ub aur khaufnaak bana deti hain. Aur phir hyena bhaag jaati hai. Aur woh... woh apne bachon ko wapas le aati hai. Aur tum log, tum log samajhte ho ke maada cheetah bhookh ke liye taaqat ke zaum mein shikaar karti hai? Aisa nahi hota Saadi. Koi apni khushi se kisi ka khoon nahi karta. Apne bachon ke liye, apni baqa ke liye woh aisa karti hai." Aur phir sir takiye par giraaye, usne aankhein moond li.

Aansoo tap tap gir rahe the. Saadi afsos se labon pe unglian rakhe use dekhta raha.

"Jao Saadi! Mujhe akela chhod do." Usne karwat badli toh woh uth khada hua.

Kuch dair baad Jawahirat ne karwat badli toh adha khula darwaza se baahir ka manzar dikhayi diya. Saadi mery engilo ke saath khada kuch keh raha tha. Unki baatein aam noiyat ki thi, woh nahi jaanti thi. Sirf Mery ki mojoodgi use bechain kar gayi. Woh kya kya bol gayi Saadi ke Saamne aur agar jo mery ne kuch bak diya to? Agar jo Saadi nay do jama do baees bana liye to? Woh uthna chahti thi, magar khawab aawar dawa ka asar gehra hota ja raha tha. Uski aankhein band hoti gayi. Zehan doobta gaya aur dil doob doob kar ubharta raha. Us se yaksar be-khabar Saadi Mery se uske maalik ki ta'ziyat kar raha tha
