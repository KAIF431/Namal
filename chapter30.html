Chapter 30

⏱️ 7 Min Read • 1,186 Words • Author: Nimra Ahmed

Logo mein gheti khadhi us siyah libaas wali ladki ki bhoori aankhon ki tapish goya andar tak utar gayi thi. Jaise us ne us ke saare andar ko jala daala ho.

Woh bilkul khamosh baitha raha. Kamre mein sannata tha. Madham zard battiyaan jal rahi thin. Bahar ke shor aur ronaq se bilkul kat kar yeh kamra khamosh sa tha.

Phir woh utha aur dheere dheere chalta khidki tak aaya. Parda zara sa sarkaaya. Samne hi lawn mein woh khadi thi.

Baddili se woh Saadi se kuch keh rahi thi. Apni tamam tar khushk mizaji aur talkhi ke bawajood uski aankhon mein utri gehri udaasi ko woh yahan se bhi dekh sakta tha.

Jaise woh andar ki saari veeraniyon ko chhupane ke liye khud pe karkhtgi ka malma chadhaye huve thi.

Us pe nazrein jamaye woh yunhi khada raha. Ungliyon se parde ko usi tarah thaame rakha. Woh manzar mein thi, aur Faris ki nigahein wahi jami thi.

Zehn mein bohat se purane manzar ghoom rahe thay."Ji haan, main poore wosooq se keh sakti hoon ke Faris Ghazi ne mujhe restaurant bulaya tha aur phir mujh par goli chalayi thi."

Barson pehle woh adalati kathere mein khadi thi aur gardan uthaye sapat andaz mein keh rahi thi."Nahi, maine usay goli chalate nahi dekha tha magar maine uski awaaz suni thi. Mai gawah hoon is baat ki ke mujh par aur Zartasha Ghazi par hamla karne wala mulzim Faris Ghazi hi tha."

Faris ne parda chhor diya. Kapra lehra kar apni jagah par aa gira. Bahar ka manzar chhup gaya.

Uska dil bura ho gaya tha. Aankhon mein nagawari ubhar aayi thi. Woh sar jhatak kar wapas bed ki taraf aaya. Tez safed batti jalayi aur phir bag kholne laga.

Kuch der pehle khidki ke paas kharay shakhs wala koi ta'assur uske chehre pe nahi tha. Wahan sirf sanjeedgi thi aur sapat pan.

Ab woh bag ki tamaam ashyaa ko ek ek kar ke dekh raha tha. Check kar raha tha.

Bahar cake kat raha tha. Hashim aur Shehrin bachhi ke gird muskurate hue mojood thay. Masnooi qabze, khokhli khushiyan.

Phir Shehrin ne cake ke tukde karna shuru kiya. Woh fondant ka teen manzila cake tha jaise asli gudiya phoolay fraak ke saath khadi ho.

Woh Frozen ki Elsa thi. Magar uska libaas neela nahi, barfila gulabi tha. Chand cakes aur bhi central table pe rakhe thay jinke ab fiona tukray kar rahi thi. Elsa wale cake pe Elsa ne ek dil utha rakha tha jismein "Soniya" likha tha.

Shehrin ne woh dil Soniya ki plate mein daala, magar jab cake serve kiya jaane laga to usne woh dil ek aur dish mein upar rakh kar kaha:"Yeh DA ki table pe le jao."

Fiona use foran wahan le aayi. DA (Zumar) to nahi thi magar Saadi ne yeh sab bohat ghoor se dekha, aur phir Shehrin ko.

Woh use hi dekh rahi thi. Magar use dekhte paakar mehmanon ki jaanib mutawajjeh ho gayi.

Yani Saadi khud samajh le to samajh le, woh bas kinare kinare reh kar hi madad karegi.

Zumar andar aayi to wahan bhi mehmaan bikhrey huay thay. Ameeron ki daawatein — saara ghar hi khol kar rakh dete hain.

"Guest washroom kis taraf hai?" Zumar ne guzarte waiter ko roka. Woh kisi kaam se aaya tha, so haath ki bajaye guest room ki taraf ishaara kar diya.

Woh seedhi udhar chali aayi. Woh aansu jo bahar mazbooti ke khol ne behne nahi diye thay, woh andar utarne ke bawajood aankhon ko surkh kar gaye thay.

Usne guest room ka darwaza dhakela ke bathroom ja kar munh dhoye — magar...

Bed pe bag khula pada tha. Ek machine gun aur pistol, goliyan — aur khud woh bed ke kinare pe jo ghutnon ke paas baith kar pindli ke saath chaaku baandh raha tha.

Aahat pe chonk kar sar uthaya. Phir wahi ruk gaya. Seedha bhi na hua.

Chaukhat pe khadi Zumar ka saans ruk gaya tha.

Uski nigaahein aslahay se hoti Faris ke chehre tak gayin. Phir unmein utra num gussa mein badla . Jabday ki ragein tan gayi. Woh peeche hui aur zor se darwaza band kiya. Ab usay mazeed fresh hone ki khwahish nahin thi. Woh tez tez chalti bahar ki taraf badh gayi.

Haneen ke kapron par cake ka tukra gira tha. Woh Saim ko liye andar aa gayi. Cake ke baad sab phir se bikhar gaye thay. Khaane mein abhi waqt tha.

Haneen ko yaad tha ke guest washrooms kidhar hain. Dakhli raste mein se ek darwaza khulta aur andar sheeshe ki deewar ke saath qataar mein basins thay.

"Kuch logon ke chehray ko dekh kar lagta hai unko bhadon ne kaata hai. Magar Nausherwan bhai ke balon ko dekh kar mujhe yahi lagta hai,"Raahdaari se guzarte Sher ko dekh kar Saim ne tabsira kiya.

Haneen ko shadeed hansi aayi magar usne zor se Saim ke chunky kaani."Apni commentary band rakho."Woh tap pe upar neeche haath maarne lagi. Woh khul nahi raha tha.

Chunanchay darwaza khula tha aur har guzarta shakhs dikhai de raha tha. Tabhi Hashim ne chaukhat pe ruk kar poocha,"Kya ho raha hai bachon?"

Haneen ne khushgawar hairat se sar uthaya. Woh unko dekh kar bilkhusoos ruka tha. Sab se hat kar bhi us se mulaqat mumkin thi? Phir jheep kar bola,"Nal nahi khul raha."

"Aahista se us ke neeche haath le jao," Hashim ne muskurate hue ishaara kiya.Haneen ne aahista se tap ke talay haath kiye. Paani ki dhaar beh padi."Oh," woh jheep gayi. Haath dho kar hatti. Dhaar ghaib."Automatic... Isay kyun bhool gayi?"

Same andar washroom ki taraf chala gaya. Haneen paper towel se haath sukhakar chaukhat tak aayi.

"Toh kya subjects hain tumhare?" Hashim ne baat ka aaghaz kiya."Literature," woh nigaahein jhuka kar jheep kar muskurayi."Oh... main samjha shayad..." Woh hairan hua tha. Haneen ke chehre pe ek saaya guzra.

Hashim ne use ghoor se dekha aur baat badal di,"Toh kya literature mein bhi naqal ho sakti hai?""Naqal har subject mein ho sakti hai, magar aapne yeh nahi poocha ke maine naqal ki thi ya nahi?"

"Main yeh bhi nahi poochta," woh muskuraya, "magar yeh zaroor poochunga ke tumhare glasses kahan gaye? Tum to chashmish hoti thin na?"

"Utar gaye. Bhai ne laser karwa diya tha," usne qadray aitmaad se Hashim ko muskara kar dekha."Aap ko meri ainak yaad hai, magar subha aapne poocha "Kaun Haneen?'" Woh halka phulka sa shikwa kar gayi.

"Kyunkay mere jaanne walon mein do aur Haneen bhi hain. Ek apne naam ke dono N ke darmiyan 'I' lagati hai, aur doosri double L.""Tum kya lagati ho?""Double E.""Good! Khair aati jaati raha karo. Soniya mein sab se milti raho... ya bhai sakhti karta hai?" Hashim ne muskara kar poocha magar woh bohat gehri nigahon se usay dekh raha tha.

"Soniya aur aap ki umar meri umar ki nahi hai. Aur bhai se acha mere liye duniya mein koi nahi hai," Woh bhi muskara kar boli, magar bhai ka elay andaz mein zikr use acha nahi laga tha.

Hashim mazeed kuch kehta magar kaan mein koi awaaz aayi. Woh maazrat karta aage badh gaya. Phir kaan mein mojood aala (device) activate hua.
