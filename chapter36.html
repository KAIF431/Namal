Chapter 36

⏱️ 6 Min Read • 1,025 Words • Author: Nimra Ahmed

Woh jo do ungliyon se kanpatti masal rahi thi, chonk kar unko dekhne lagi.

“Kya kehna chaah rahe hain aap?”

“Woh dhokebaaz hai. Us se fareb ki hi tawaqqo karo, Zumar!”Unki awaaz buland hone lagi. Alfaaz se zyada lehja mukhtalif tha—ajeeb tha, hila dene wala tha.

“Mat kahe… kuch mat kahe…”Aur woh matwahhish ho kar unko rokna chahti thi. Woh kuch nahi sunna chahti thi.

“Tumne usse kaha ke woh tumhari takleef nahi samajh sakta. Zahir hai, woh kaise samajh sakta hai? Usne to tab bhi tumhe dhoka hi diya tha!”Zumar ke lab adhh khulay reh gaye. Toote kaanch se uska dil zakhmi kiya ja raha tha.

Bade Abba apni jagah se aage huay, zara jhukay. Zumar ki aankhon mein jhaank kar kehne lage:

“Yaad hai woh European aurat jisne tumhein gurda diya tha?”Zumar ne sar bhi asbaat mein naa hilaya. Woh bas unko dekh rahi thi.

“Zumar! Us aurat ne gurda nahi diya tha… tumhe woh gurda Saadi ne diya tha.”

Woh ek dam khadhi hui… phir mudhi… khidki ke patt zor se dhakelay.Taaza hawa mein, damay ke mareez ki tarah muh khol kar, aankhein band kar ke saans lene ki koshish ki.

“Woh ladka kitna jhoota hai na… usne tumse jhoot bola. Dhoka diya. Sab usne plan kiya tha. Uska khoon, gurda—sab tumhare jaisa tha. Magar dil tumse bada tha. Woh kehta tha, ‘yeh mera niyat hai… main teemardaari karke number bana li ya padhai ke bahaane nazron se gayab ho kar apna farz ada karu… aur agar bura banta hoon to ban jau… magar is test mein fail nahi hona chahiye mujhe…’”

“Kamar ko kaat kar gurda nikaalne ki takleef kya hoti hai, Zumar… usko pata hai. Woh ladka aaj ek gurde par hai. Jab tum hospital mein thi, to woh bhi qareebi kamray mein admit tha. Magar usse to hamdardi bhi nahi mili. Woh chaar saal se khamoshi se tumhari sard mehri bardasht karta aa raha hai. Aur tum kehti ho, woh tumhari takleef nahi samajhta?”

Usne tezi se saans lete huve aankhein kholi. Uska rang safed pad raha tha… shayad ab woh neela padne wala tha.Sirf dame se hi rang neela nahi padta…

“Mujhe kyun nahi bataya?”Ruk ruk kar alfaaz nikle. Usse saans nahi liya ja raha tha. Woh khidki ko pakde khadi thi. Thakan se palke band ho rahi thi.

“Bohat khuddar hai mera beta, Zumar! Maine kitni minnat ki thi uski… magar woh kehta tha, ‘agar phupo ko pata chala ke yeh mera gurda hai to woh kabhi nahi lenge. Phupo mujhse bohat mohabbat karti hain. Mai unka bhai bhi hoon, dost bhi, beta bhi… woh mujhe takleef se nahi guzarna de sakti. Aise woh bhi theek nahi hongi.’”

“Main aaj bhi na batata agar tum raat usko yeh na jataati…”Usne karb se aankhein band kar lein.Gurda dene ki takleef zyada badi thi ya dil katne ki—iss sawal ko to jawab ki zarurat hi nahi thi.

Woh pad-murda, naheef chehre ke saath uski pusht dekh rahe thay.“Agar aaj tumhare paas ek gurda hai… to uski wajah Saadi hai.”

Woh dheere se palti… uski aankhon ki gulabi lakeerein ab surkh ho chuki thi. Shayad unmein nami bhi thi.Bhale woh unhe na girne deti… magar woh har haal mein aansu thai

“Aap yeh keh rahe hain ke agar aaj us ke paas ek gurda hai to uski wajah mai hu?”

Aur yeh koi sawaal nahi tha, so iska koi jawab bhi nahi tha. Woh nam aankhon se usko dekhte rahe. Jawab ka intezar usse bhi nahi tha. Woh tezi se apne kamre ki taraf chali gayi. Khidki ab poori khul chuki thi, aur taaza hawa bohot umeed afza thi.

“Ulfat ke saude kaun kare, nafrat ki jholi kaun bhare, hum karobari duniya mein begaane hi begaane hain…”

Siyah BMW us banglay ke porch mein ruki. Chauffeur ne foran darwaza khola. Hashim bahar nikla aur Sonia ki ungli pakad kar usay bhi bahar laya. Phir glasses utaar kar, girebaan mein latkate hue, daakhli darwaze ko dekha jahan Shehreen khadi thi.

Woh abhi wahi thi, magar bob cut baal bilkul set thay.“Bye Baba!” Sonia se milne ko woh jhuka to usne baap ke dono gaal choome.Phir peeche utarte Nosherwan ko haath hilaaya.

“Hi Sherwa!”Woh jo khashmgeen nigahon se sirf Shehreen ko dekh raha tha, badqist muskara kar sar ko kham diya.Sonia bhaagti hui maa ke galay lag gayi jo uske liye jhuki thi un dono se qatan be-niyaz.

"Mera Baby" Aankhein moonde, bachi ko saath lagaye bar-baraayi.Hashim ek haath jaib mein daale muskara kar dono ko dekh raha tha.

“Bataya hai mujhe Sonia ne raste mein… ke usay kitni khwahish thi hamare ‘Honey Moon’ ki tasveere dekhne ki.”

Shehreen be-ikhtiyar seedhi hui. Nigaahein phisal kar khud ko chhupate aankhon se dekhte hue Sheru par gayi.Uski gardan mein gulti ubhar kar ma’doom hui.

“Toh.”To woh bazahir la-parwah thi. Sonia ko sar ke ishaaray se andar bheja.

“To tumhe lagta tha tum mujhe bewaqoof bana logi?”Woh muskurate hue aage aaya. Uske bilkul muqabil khadha hua aur aankhon mein dekh kar bola:

“Tum kya keh rahe ho?” Woh uktai.

“Shehreen! Insaan mein itne guts hone chahiye ke apne amal ki zimmedari le. Tum se acha to Saadi nikla. Do haath lagaye mere guard ne to sab bak diya ke kis tarah tum ne usay password diya. Aur haan, woh bhi meri hi beti ke cake par! Tum achi jasoos ban sakti ho waise. Tumne ISI ke liye apply kyun nahi kiya?”

Shehreen ke abroo hairat se uthe.“Saadi ne…?”

“Oh, tumhe laga tha woh nahi batayega?”Shehrin ki aankhon mein gussa aur bezari ubhri.

“Main tum se itni ukta chuki hu ke tumhare khilaf madad maangne wale ko inkaar nahi kar sakti… aur kisi ache dost ko to bilkul nahi.”

“Acha dost?”“Kya tumne note kiya?” Woh mudhe baghair Nosherwan se poochhne laga.

Aur usko doosri dafa sadma hua tha. Abhi tak umeed thi ke shayad… magar ab nahi. Gham ghusse mein badalne laga. Woh bhai ke aqab se nikal kar aage aaya.

“Kya tumhe mai hi mila tha istimaal karne ke liye?" Bhawain bheenche woh gusse se keh raha tha. "Woh bhi us loser Saadi ke liye? Usko toh mai chodunga nahi aur badla tumse bhi lunga"
