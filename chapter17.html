CHAPTER 17

Chapter 17

⏱️ 6 Min Read • 1,001 Words • Author: Nimra Ahmed

Haneen khidki ka parda sarkaaye baahar dekh rahi thi, door sadak par jaise kisi ko talaash kar rahi ho... kisi ke bhool ke wapas aane ka intezaar kar rahi ho.

Saadi ki aankhon mein udaasi aur labon pe muskurahat dhar aayi. Woh khamoshi se wahan se hat gaya. Rahdaari mein wapas chalte hue usne haath mein pakray siyah aur sunahre card ko dekha.

Ek manzar uski aankhon ke samne jhalmilaya...

Hotel ki lobby zard roshniyon mein chamak rahi thi. Chaar paanch suit mein malboos afraad khushgawar andaaz mein ek doosre se mil rahe the. Un mein ek Hashim Kardaar bhi tha jo kisi se muskara ke kuch keh raha tha. Hashim ke peechhe uski secretary khadi thi, jisne ek haath mein Hashim ka laptop uthaya hua tha aur woh haath pehlu mein gira hua tha.

Woh bhi saamne muskurate hue meeting ke liye aaye afraad ko dekh rahi thi. Door se jeans, shirt aur cap mein malboos Saadi chalta hua aaya. Uska sar jhuka hua tha. Woh usi tarah secretary ke paas se guzra aur aage badh gaya.

Secretary mein mutawajjeh hi nahi hui-usne dekha hi nahi ke ladke ke guzarte hi laptop ke side ke socket mein ek flash drive lag chuki thi.

Saadi ek qareebi table par ja baitha. Kandhe se bag utara, andar se tablet nikala aur us par mukhtalif jagahon par ungli se press karne laga.

Screen par yeh paighaam aaraha tha:"Aap ki device ko ek hard drive mili hai. Kya aap saara data copy karna chahein ge?"

Saadi ne muskurate hue "Yes" dabaya.

Agle hi lamhe uski muskurahat gaib ho gayi. Screen par paighaam jhal-mila raha tha:"Password daakhil karein."

"Oh nahi yaar..." Usne bebasi se mud kar dekha jahan woh log abhi tak kharay baat kar rahe the.Use kyun khayal nahi aaya ke Hashim ke laptop par password ho sakta hai?

Woh jaldi se sab samaan samet kar utha aur sar jhukaye un ke qareeb se guzra-aur secretary se takra gaya."Sorry..." khafiif sa keh kar aage badh gaya.

Hashim ne chonk kar use dekha aur phir door tak sochti nigahon se uska ta'aqub kiya.

"Chali gayin?" Faaris ki awaaz par Saadi chonka.Uske saamne Faaris khada tha.

"Haan." Usne card badhaya, jaise phuppo ke aane ka maqsad bayaan kiya ho.

Faaris ne sarsari sa dekha aur phir gol mez tak aagaya. Haneen, Usama sab wapas aa gaye. Zara si halchal ke baad zindagi jaise phir normal routine par aagayi thi.

"Ab na fursat hai, na ehsaas hai gham se apne..."

Aasmaan par siyaahi phail rahi thi.

Woh study table pe files phelaaye baithi thi. Halkee si aahat ne usey sar uthane pe majboor kiya.

Ab woh wheelchair mein andar aa rahe the.

Woh be-ikhtiyaar khadi ho gayi."Aap ke bulaane pe na aati to aap khud aa gaye?"

Rasaan se shikwa karke woh wheelchair peechay se thaamay samne laayi, aur phir khud muqaabil hone pe paon upar karke baith gayi.

Baday Abba muntazir nigahon se use dekh rahe thay...

"Kya usne khana nahi poocha jo shaam mein tum ne wapas aakar khaya?""Maine yeh nahi kaha tha ke main kha kar aaungi. Khana matter nahi karta," ghunghriali lat ungli par let'te usne jawab diya.

"Kya woh khush tha?""Aap ko din mein do dafa to phone karta hi hai, pooch lijiye ga."

Phir dono ke beech khidki ke baahar pheli raat jaisi khamoshi chha gayi. Abba fikrmandi aur ta'assuf se usse dekh rahe the.

"Phir baat aap ne shuru karni hai ya maine? Aur agar aap ne ki to kitne fiqron ki tamheed baandhein ge?"Usne itminaan se poocha.

"Zumar... shaadi kar lo." Woh azurda se bole.

"Aaj aap ne tamheed hi nahi baandhi."Usne cushion utha kar god mein rakha.

"Kab tak is toote rishte ka soag manao gi meri bachi? Meri maut aasaan kar do, ab bas kar do."

"Aap jaante hain main jazbaati blackmailing mein nahi aaya karti. Jab mujhe karni hogi, main bata dungi. Waise bhi ab main boodhi ho rahi hoon, kaun karega mujhse shaadi?"

"Do chaar saal mein waqai boodhi lagne lago gi. Main is takleef ke saath nahi marna chahta."

"Okay Abba, saaf baat karte hain."Usne cushion pare rakha, pair neeche kiye, taang par taang jamayi, baal kaanon ke peeche ud se, aur gehri saans li.

Woh wapas District Prosecutor ke roop mein chali gayi thi.

"Aap meri shaadi kisi bhi XYZ se kara dein, main kar loongi. Phir chand din mein mazeed bad-dil ho jaungi. Zyada bezar aur talkh. Woh mujhse expectations bandhega jo main poori nahi karungi. Main aisi hi rahungi. Woh shuru mein bardasht karega, kahega maazi bhool do. Main kahungi shaadi jab ki, tab bhi us 'ghair' se nahi nikli thi, abhi waqt lagega. Woh sabr kar lega, magar phir jald hi sabr kho dega, ghussa karega, haath uthaye ga, nafrat karega, teen maheene mein ghar se nikaal dega aur main yahin aa kar baithi hoongi."

"Ab batayein, aap ke liye kya zyada takleef deh hoga?"

Abba ne dukh se usse dekha."Kya tum apni shaadi ko kaamiyab banane ki koi koshish nahi karogi?"

"Us phase se nikli hi nahi to kaise karungi?"

"Kab niklo gi us 'phase' se?"

"Aap mujhe jaante hain. Jab mere upar kuch taari ho jaye to mere liye usay jhatakna namumkin hota hai. Main usi ko apni zindagi bana leti hoon. Aur jab aakhri dafa humne yeh behas ki thi to do din tak ek doosre se baat nahi ki thi. Is dafa kitne din ka irada hai?"

"Haan... kitne din?"Abba ne aahista se asbaat mein sar hilaaya.

"Magar tum koshish to karogi na us phase se nikalne ki?"

"Main chaar saal se koshish kar rahi hoon. Main bohot trauma se guzri hoon. Mere gurday zaya ho gaye, tayar shaadi cancel ho gayi, woh Hammad mujhe chhod kar chala gaya. Bimari ke aalam mein woh waqt bohot bura tha, Abba. Main aage barh nahi sakti jab tak us waqt ko bhula na doon. Mujhe kuch time dein."

Woh sar hilate hue wapas palat gaye. Zumar khamoshi se unko jaate dekhti rahi, magar woh khud bhi be-bas thi.
