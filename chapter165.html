CHAPTER 165

Chapter 165

⏱️ 5 Min Read • 722 Words • Author: Nimra Ahmed

Bhoolne wala laut to aaya. waqt maghrib ya isha ka tha.

Chhote baagiche waale ghar mein kitchen se pakte khaane ki mehek yun phel rahi thi jaise paani ke glass mein tapka ink ka boond phelta ho, aur poora mahaul khushbu se mahka diya ho. Aise mein Haneen, Saadi ke khaali kamre mein be-maqsad kursi pe baithi thi. Kohniyan mez par lagaayi, chehra hatheliyon pe gira diya. Aankhon se chashma utaar kar side par rakh diya. Kuch dair ungli se mez par lakeerein khinchtee rahi, phir achanak chonk gayi.

Karib mein safed jild waali kitaab rakhi thi. Saath mein wrapper aur card bhi tha. Saadi woh kitaab kisi ko tohfa dene waala tha? Hairani se usne card uthaya. Saalgirah ka card, Zumar ke naam. Ohh, Phupho ki saalgirah thi na kuch din baad, toh Saadi woh kitaab Zumar ko dene jaa raha tha. Yeh wahi kitaab thi jo barson pehle usne ek dafa yunhi khol li thi. Ab dubara kholi toh pehle safhe pe Hashim ka naam likha tha. Usne naam par ungli pheri aur muskurai. Phir be-maqsad safhe palatne lagi.

Achanak beech mein ek panna par ruk gayi. Saat so baras purane zard zamaane ko jaata ek darwaza saamne tha. Haneen ne thoda socha ki andar jaaye ya nahi, phir bina zyada soche haath badhaya aur dhakka diya. Lakdi ke purane naqsh daar darwaze khul gaye. Wahan se roshni ka toofan umad aaya. Aankhein chundhiya gayi.

Roshni thodi kam hui toh usne palkein jhapka jhapka kar idhar-udhar dekha. Woh qadeem Dimishq (Damascus) ke us zard se makaan ke bahar khadi thi jo masjid se mila hua tha. Ek zamaane mein usne yahan majma mein ghire ek beemaar ko dekha tha. Aaj yahan virani thi, sannata tha. Shaam ki zardi utar rahi thi. Roshni ab khatam ho chuki thi. Makaan ke andar chirag jal rahe the. Pajama, lambi qameez, aur hair band lagaaye baalo waali Haneen us poore zard manzar mein ek hi rangeen cheez thi. Usne pehle idhar-udhar dekha. Phir billi ki chaal chalti, phoonk phoonk ke qadam rakhti makaan ke andar aayi. Pehle kamre ka parda hataaya aur sir neecha karke andar daakhil hui.

Is mutala'ati (study) kamre mein jagah jagah diye jal rahe the ya kuch moti mom battiyan. Deewar mein bane khaanon mein kitaabein rakhi thi. Samne farsh par do zaano hokar Sheikh Muallim baithe the, aur chowki par dhare kaagaz par qalam  siyahi mein dubo dubo kar likhte ja rahe the. Woh seene par baazu lapete, chokhat mein khadi tanqeedi nazron se unhein dekh rahi thi. Phir dil mazid mazboot kar ke pukara:

"Kya aapne apni kitaab khatam nahi ki?" Woh sir jhukaaye likhte rahe. Haneen ne aankhein narazi se sikodi. Aas paas sab kuch zardi mein dooba hua tha, jaise purane zamaane ka koi print ho aur ek wahi colourful thi. Phir qadam qadam chalti woh qareeb aayi. Chowki ke bilkul saamne. Sir jhuka kar jaise jhaank rahi ho.

"Kya aapki kitaab mein waqai dil ki bimariyo ka ilaaj hai?" Puchte waqt chehra yunhi be-niyaaz banaya jaise jawab mein koi dilchaspi hi na ho, magar saari haseeyat jawab par lagi hui thi.

"Har marz ki dawa hai... jo use jaanta hai. Woh ise jaanta hai. Aur jo use nahi jaanta..." Sir jhukaaye likhte hue unhone jawab diya.

"Ahh! Aapke zamane ke marz!" Usne goya mayoosi se haath jhaade. Phir samne baith gayi, choki pe kohni rakhi aur thori phela ke gira di. "Ta'aun aur doosre wabai marz humare zamane mein nahi hote. Humare masle aur hain, you know... magar nahi, aapko kya pata," phir jaise use gussa aagaya. Tewri chadha kar boli, "Aap saat so saal qadeem ke ek boodhe hain... ek naive boodhe. Aapko to ye tak nahi maloom ke computer kya hota hai, internet kya hota hai, TV shows kise kehte hain... aur woh zindagi kaise tabah kar dete hain. Magar nahi! Uff!" Jaise kraah kar sar jhatka. Afsos se unko dekha.

"Aapki kitaab meri madad nahi kar sakti, kyunki isme mere kisi masle ka hal nahi hai."

Woh hanuz qalam siyahi mein duba duba kar likhte ja rahe thay to zuch ho kar Haneen unke parche pe jhuki. Gardan tirchi kar ke padha:

"Aey logon jo imaan laye ho! Beshak khamr, maysar, ansaab, azlaam, yeh sab shaitaan ke gande kaam hain. Toh in se bacho taake tum kamiyabi pao."

(Surah Al-Ma'idah: Ayat 90)
