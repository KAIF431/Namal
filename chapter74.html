Chapter 74

⏱️ 8 Min Read • 1,292 Words • Author: Nimra Ahmed

Kabhi kabhi aarzoo ke sehra mein aaye rukte hain qafle se.

Subah Haneen hamesha ki tarah  bhaag-daudh karte huve school ke liye taiyar ho rahi thi. Zumar aur Saim bilkul taiyar uska intezar kar rahe the darwaze par khade the. Idhar woh aayi, udhar ghanti baji. Zumar ne aage badh kar darwaza khola. Ek naujawan bahar khada tha. Suit mein mablus, sunglasses lagaye, haath mein lamba sa dabba.  "Haneen Yousuf?"Woh sawaliya nazron se use dekhti ek taraf ho gayi.

"Kardar sahab ne bhijwaya hai."Woh unka koi mulazim tha. Packet hawale kar ke modab sa palat gaya.

Bahar uski car khadi thi. Haneen qadar e hairan, qadar e anjaan si dabba le kar andar aayi. Gole maiz par usse rakha. Sab ird-gird ikatthe ho gaye. Usne zara tazabzub se dabba uthaya aur phir... saans lena bhool gayi. Naya-nakoor laptop, iPad, iPhone, iPod har modern aala alag-alag dabbe mein tha. Aur unke upar ek note.

"Mai kisi ka ehsan nahi bhoolta."   -Aurangzeb

Zumar ne note padha. Nudarat ne aahista se usse bataya ke woh kaun hai.

"Faris ka woh cousin Hashim, jiska Saadi aksar zikr karta hai."

"Okay!" Woh Haneen ke ta'suraat dekhne lagi. Jo shock se nikal kar khushi se sab kholne lagi. Nudarat albata chup ho gayi.  "Itne mehange-mehange gifts? Yeh humein nahi rakhne chahiye."

Zumar Haneen ko le kar darwaze ki taraf badh gayi. Woh unki itni zaati si guftagu mein dakhal nahi dena chahti thi. Nikalte waqt usne Haneen ki awaaz suni.  "Ammi, Yaar kya hai? Maine unka laptop theek kiya tha. Woh shukriya karna chah rahe hai. Aise kaise wapas?"  Woh bahar aa gayi. Jab Hannah car mein aayi aur front seat par baithi toh apni ammi ka mobile kaan se lagaye baat kar rahi thi. Zumar ko maloom tha kis ki call hogi. Uski aadhi raat hogi na! Usne muskara kar car start ki magar woh sune bagair purjosh tafseelat bata rahi thi.

"Laptop silver color ka hai aur iPod..."    "Meri baat suno, Hannah! Tum yeh sab wapas kar do."  Woh neend se uth chuka tha aur ab mukammal alert tha. Woh bolte-bolte ruk gayi. Zumar ne drive karte huve ek nazar us par daali.

"Yeh sab mai tumhein le doon ga."

"Aur agar tab main aapko wapas kar doon toh aapko kya lagega, bhai?" "Unhone mujhe gareeb rishtedaar samajh kar taras kha kar nahi diya. Maine unka kaam kiya tha, Unhone shukriya ada kiya hai. Agar main tohfay lene ki laalchi hoti toh jab woh kabhi-kabhi poochte hai ke falan mulk ja raha hoon, tumhein kuch chahiye? Toh har dafa yeh keh kar inkaar na karti ke 'Sorry uncle, mai baghair wajah ke tohfay nahi leti.”

"Oh acha," Woh waqai samajh gaya.

"Okay, tum rakh lo. Ab mujhe sone do."  Haneen ne phone rakh diya aur khidki ke bahar dekhne lagi. Phir qadar uljhi si Zumar ko dekha.

"Agar aapko koi aise tohfa de toh aap rakh lein gi?"

Woh apne amal ki safayi chahti thi. Zumar ko jaise kuch yaad aa gaya. Usne gear ke paas pichla khana khola aur kuch nikal kar uski god mein rakha siyah makhmali dabbi aur ek teh shuda kagaz.  "Haneen Yousuf..."  Woh sun reh gayi.

"Kal subah yeh kisi ne mujhe courier kiya tha. Padho."  Haneen ka chehra faqh ho gaya. Usne darte darte Zumar ki shakal dekhi. Jo pur-sukoon drive kar rahi thi. Usne dhadakte dil se kagaz uthaya.  Jewellery tak theek tha... Mamu se love letter ki tawakku nahi thi...  Usne kagaz ki tahe kholi.  Sar uthaya.

"Pehle kehne ki himmat nahi hui class  mein kabhi. Yeh aap par us se zyada suit  karegi jo aap pehenti hai."

"Isey love letter kehte hai? Isey achha love letter toh Lincoln Bros likh leta!"

Khat ki likhayi woh saaf pehchaan gayi. Khauf zail hua, lekin uljhan se sar uthaya.

"Kya aap yeh nose pin pehn rakhengi?"  Zumar ne chonk kar usse dekha.

"Tumne toh abhi ise khola bhi nahi!"

"Isme likha hai ke yeh aap par suit nahi karta."  Usne naak ko ungli se chhua.

"Agar kisi ka itna sense hai toh yeh bhi maloom hoga ke nose pin zyada achi lagegi."

"Ab dekhein, mera guess theek lagta hai ya nahi!"  Kehte hi dabbi kholi.

Heere ki long saamne thi. Haneen ne fatahna muskurahat se dekha. Zumar ne  kandhe ucha diye.

"Kya aapko maloom hai yeh kisne bheji hai?"  Usne ehtiyaat se poocha.  "Itne batches padhaye hai... Sauon  students guzre hai... Magar bohot kam ladkiyon ko mere ghar ka pata maloom hai. Inhi mein se koi hogi."

"Hogi?" Haneen ka halak tak kadhwa ho gaya.

"To ab aap kya karen gi ?"

"Is courier company par jakar wapsi ka pata laine ki koshish karoon gi. Aakhir unhon ne bhi kaise diamonds jewellery courier honay di. Phir is ko wapas karoongi. Kyu ke mai students se tohfe nahi leti. Yeh mere usoolo ke khilaaf hai."    "Toh phir mai bhi Kardar sahab ko yeh sab wapas kar deti hoon. Mere bhi kuch usool hone chahiye. Baat khatam. "

Haneen ne zara khafgi se kaghazdabi mein rakha. Dabbi wapas rakhi aur bahar dekhne lagi. Zumar ne gehri saans li. Haneen aur apne darmiyan taaza taaza takaloufki khaleej mein anay wali kami ko ek usool ke peeche?

"Um hmm, Usoolo mein tarmeem ho sakti hai. apno ke liye sab ho sakta hai."  "Okay, mein isay rakh leti hu"

Haneen sar hilaa kar bahar dekhti rahi. Zumar ne achambe se usay dekha. "Tum kyu muskura rahi ho?"Us ne garbara kar jabra sidha kiya aur gardan dayen baen ghumayi.

"Nahi toh"aur mazeed rukh phair liya. School mein woh dono ek sutoon ke sath khadhi huvi thi. Nigahein gate Par markaz thi.

"Humein sirf un ka address chahiye ya koi Doosri contact information."

"Woh rahi Sabreena." Usne andar aati ladki ki taraf ishara kiya, Phir bechaini se Zumar ko dekha.

"Magar aap is ka number pata kaise haasil karegi? Is ke liye toh aap ko record room mein jana ho ga ya school ke data system kaha ja rahi hai aap?"

Woh satoon ki oot se nikal kar jane lagi thi, Haneen ke har baranay par rukkar usay dekha. halka sa muskarayi.

"Sabreena se uska pata lene." Aur hakka bakka khadhi Haneen ko chor karzara aage aayi. Tab tak Sabreena baraamde tak aa chuki thi. Haneen fauran ghoom gayi. Samaat wahi lagi thi. Zumar Sabreena ke paas se guzarne lagi phir uska chehra dekh kar ruki aur Khushgawar hairat se usay pukara

"Arey! Sabreena! madam Yasmeen ki beti hona aap? kaisi ho? Madam kaisi hai?" Sabreena ruki Zara uljha uljha sa muskarayi.

"Jee mein Sabreena aap?"

"Don't tell me ke tumne mujhe nahi pehchana? Bachpan mein tum kitni  healthy thi magar ab zyada pyari hogayi ho. ammi kidhar hain? Abhi job karti hai?"

"Jee ammi vise principal."

"Kitni out of touch ho gayi ho. Mai bhi Dubai chali gayi thi na. Abhi bhatije ke admission ke liye aayi thi. Aisa karo mujhe apna number de do."

Kandhe pe tange purse se jaldi jaldi note buk aur qalam nikaal kar usay thamaya. "Land line bhi dena aur address bhi dedo. Mai madam se milne aaunge  kisi din."Sabreena ko sonchne ka zyada waqt nahi mila. Woh kaghaz pe alfaaz ghaseetne lagi jab woh door chali gayi toh Zumar satoon tak wapas aayi kaghez Haneen ke samne lehrate hue fatihana nazron se usay dekha. Woh waqai muthahayyar khadhi thi.

"Tumne abhi meri yeh wali side dekhi nahi thi Hannah!"

"Waqayi zabardast performance thi!"  Phir woh hairan pareshaan assembly ke liye bhagi, magar thehar kar mud gayi.

"Yeh" Naak par ungli rakhi"Aap par waqayi itni suit nahi karti"Aur bhaag gayi

Zumar ne car mein waapis baithte huve ek lamhe aaine mein apna chehra dekha. Sone ke baali jaisi nath... Kya waqayi us par suit nahi karti?

"Um hmm.." Usay maayusi huvi
