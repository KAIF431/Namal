Chapter 31

⏱️ 6 Min Read • 978 Words • Author: Nimra Ahmed

"Haan Khawar, bolo!"

"Sir! Aap wahi rukiye, mai aa raha hoon."Khawar lawn mein tha aur udhar aa raha tha. Hashim wahi ruk gaya, magar phir koi aur mil gaya toh woh unka haal chaal poochhne khara ho gaya. Khawar muntazir sa khadha raha. Woh farigh ho kar apne Chief Security Officer ki taraf mudha.

"Kya hua?" — Istifsaar mein sakhti thi.

"Aap ko yeh dekhna chahiye," Khawar ne tablet aage kiya. Uski screen par paanch cameras ki footage chal rahi thi. Khawar ne ek par ungli rakh kar use bada kiya.

Hashim ne aankhein sukhedh kar dekha — yeh uske kamray ke band darwazay ka manzar tha. Khawar ne usay tezi se rewind kiya aur phir play kiya.

Seedhiyon se do chaar log utarte chadhte dikhayi de rahe thai. Unme ek siyah suit aur ghungraale baalon wala ladka bhi tha jo sar jhukaaye zine philandta upar gaya. Hashim ke kamre ka darwaza khola aur andar jaakar darwaza band kar diya.

Hashim ko laga jaise kisi ne uske muh par darwaza de maara ho. Uski aankhon mein surkhi ubhri, muthiyaan bheench gayin.

"Yeh kitni der pehle ki hai?"

"Terah minute!"

Aur terah minute qabl, jab woh Hashim ke kamray mein aaya, toh usne laptop mein flash lagane mein teen second bhi nahi lagaye thai. Laptop band raha magar flash ki batti chamakne lagi. Usne panjon ke bal carpet par baith kar tezi se tab khola.

"Aap ki device ka rabta ek hard drive se ho chuka hai. Kya aap tamaam data copy karna chahein ge?"Bohat khushi ke saath, dhadakte dil se usne 'Yes' dabaya. Password usne 'Sonia' type kiya.

Har signal pe Saadi ne aankhen band kar ke gehri saans li. Data copy hona shuru ho gaya.

10%... 20%... 40%...Woh baar baar muzterib nigahon se band darwazay ko dekhta.

55%...

Neeche kharay Hashim ne sholay bar aankhon se Khawar ko dekha.

"Terah minute se woh mere kamray mein hai, aur tum ab bakwas kar rahe ho?" Woh daba daba sa garja.Khawar thook nigalte peeche hua.

"Sir, aap kisi se baat kar rahe—""Do bando ko le kar meri balcony pe jao. Main idhar se jaata hoon."

Sari shaistagi, mehmaan-nawazi dafaan kar ke woh tez tez zine tak gaya.

70%... 73%...Saadi bechaini se ungliyaan marodh raha tha.Hashim coat ka button kholte hue zine philand raha tha — kisi aandhi toofan ki tarah. Jaise abhi jaa kar Saadi ko girebaan se dabocha lena chahta ho.Us ullu ke paththe ne Hashim bhai ko abhi bohat underestimate kiya tha.

85%... 90%...Saadi ne flash ungliyon se pakar rakha tha. Ginti khatam ho aur woh use kheench le. Maatha paseene se bheeg raha tha.

Hashim ne dhaar se darwaza khola.Gusse se bhari uski nigahein aage peechhe daudne lage

Kamra khaali tha. Saadi wahan nahi tha. Albata hilta hua parda hata hua tha. Balcony ka darwaza poora khula tha. Woh andha dhundh baahar bhaaga. Balcony mein bhi woh nahi tha. Woh tezi se bairooni zeene utarne laga. Uss taraf lawn khaali aur neem andhera tha. Khawar aur do suit pehne aadmi bhaagte hue udhar aa rahe thay. Hashim ka maatha bheegne laga — "Woh kahan gaya?"

Andar khaali kamray mein harkat huvi. Bathroom ka darwaza khol kar Saadi aahista se nikla aur usi aahistagi se kamray se baahar aakar darwaza band kar diya.

"Kya hai Hashim bhai! Aaj kal ke bachay thode se zyada smart hai," kaan khujate hue usne masoomiyat se khud kalami ki aur usi itminan se seerhiyaan utarne laga.

Dakhli darwazay ke qareeb deewar par bohat se digital photo frames aawizaan thai. Un mein tasaveer slideshow ki soorat mein harkat kar rahi thein. Haneen aur Saim baatein karte hue kaafi shauq se unko dekh rahe thay — Hashim, Nausherwaa waghera ki tasaveer — bachpan, university.

Saadi abhi seedhiya utar kar aaya hi tha ke—

"Oye Saadi!"Nosherwan, jo jaibon mein haath daale ek mujassmay se tek lagaye khadha tha, pukar kar bola. Saadi ghooma. Woh aadatan baghair coat ke, sunehri shirt par siyah waist mein malboos tha aur istehzai muskaraah se use dekh raha tha.

"Apne behan bhai ko le aaya karo naa kabhi idhar. Dekho kitne excited ho rahe hain. Unho ne shayad aisi cheezein pehle nahi dekhi."

Saadi ne ek nazar door khadhe dono par daali.

"Haan! Unho ne tum jaisi cheezein kam hi dekhi hain."Magar Nosherwan ne jaise nahi suna.

"Magar unka qasoor nahi hai. Ghurbat aur chhota khandan bohat badi museebat hai," ta'asuf se kehte hue usne sar hilaya.

"Agar tum yeh chahte ho ke main bhadak kar tum par hamla karu aur tum sab mein mera tamasha banao, toh aisa nahi hoga. Main mehmaan hoon. Aadaab hoga. Mehmaani mujhe aati hai," sanjeedgi se keh kar woh mud gaya. Uska rukh dakhli darwazay ki simt tha.

"Tumhari behan kaafi badi ho gayi hai," Nosherwan ne phir pukara. Ab ke hamla mukhtalif noiyat ka tha.

Saadi ke qadam zanjeer hue. Usne gardan modhi. Aankhon mein surkhi ubhri, lab bheenche magar us se pehle ke woh jhapatt kar bhinche hue muthi ko Nosherwan ke chehre tak le jaata—

"Oye! Kya bola hai? Kis ki behan ki baat ki hai haan?"Faris barhmi se bolta tez tez qadam uthata udhar aa raha tha. Aise ke woh jo Saadi se do inch lamba tha, Saadi ke aage aakar Nosherwan ki taraf badha.

Nosherwan waqai ghabraya tha. Usne Faris ko aate nahi dekha tha, magar la parwai se shanay jhatkay.

"Aisa kya keh diya maine?""Bakwas mat karo! Meri behan ki beti ka naam mat lena aindah... warna haath paon salamat nahi rahenge tumhare. Baat samajh mein aayi ya nahi? Haan?"Ghoortay hue ungli se uske seene ko dhakela.

Tab hi Hashim ne aakar tezi se dono haathon se dono ko door kiya. Woh abhi abhi seerhiyaan utarta udhar aaya tha.

"Kya masla hai? Kya hua hai?" Sulah-jo andaaz mein usne Faris ka kandha thaama.Magar Faris ne jhatke se chhuda liya aur gusse bhari nigahon se Hashim ko dekha.
