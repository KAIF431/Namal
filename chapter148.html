"Jab tak woh mujhe maaf nahi kar dete."  "Toh tum unse maafi maang lo na, simple.' Mulazimo ki zabani woh sab sun chuki thi.

"Kitni dafa maang chuka hoon, magar jawab mein sirf cheekh-chilla kar mujhe dafa kar dete hain."

"Aur Hashim?" Usne plate se phal ka tukda uthakar munh mein daala.  "Woh toh mujhse baat bhi nahi kar raha."

"Aur tumne isi liye use ek dafa bhi mukhatib nahi kiya?... Khaaoge?" Shehreen ne saath hi plate badhaayi. Nausherwan ne be-dili se munh pher liya, albata ab woh Shehreen se pehle ki tarah bezaar nahi rehta tha. Sirf wahi thi jisne saara qissa sunne ke baad usse hamdardi jatayi thi aur kaha tha, "Bhai, tumne lalach mein toh nahi kiya na? Ek adventure tha ye, ismein itna naraaz hone wali kya baat hai?"    Ab bhi woh kandhe uchka kar keh rahi thi, "Yun karo, downstairs jao aur Hashim se maafi maang lo. Baat khatam. Usko sirf tumhari maafi ka intezaar hai." "Waqai?" Usne bechaini se Shehreen ko dekha, woh thappad phir yaad aaya. Beikhtiyaar gaal pe haath rakha.

"Hana. Woh tumse kabhi khafa nahi ho sakta aur mujhe apna phone de jao."

"Kyun?" Woh phone dete dete ruka.  Shehreen ne mobile uske haath se achanak le liya.

"Waqt zaya mat karo, woh office ke liye nikal hi na jaye."

"Accha." Woh foran upar aaya. Thodi der uske kamre ke baahar ruka raha. Peeche seedhiyon pe baithi Shehreen ne uske mobile se Saadi ka number nikala aur apne phone mein transfer kiya.  Shero ne bina khatkhataye darwaza khola. Hashim dressing mirror ke saamne khada tha. Coat abhi stand pe tha aur do cufflinks pehn raha tha. Aahat par gardan mori, usse dekha, aur wapas cufflink pehnne laga.    "Aao Shero." Andaaz normal tha. na gussa, na pyaar. Woh sir jhukaye, lab kaat-ta, qadam-qadam chalta kareeb aaya. Yeh us din ke baad dono ki pehli baat-cheet thi. Yeh social boycott uske liye bohot takleef-deh sabit hua tha.

"Bhai... abhi tak naraaz hain mujhse?" Nigah uthane ki himmat nahi hui. Hashim ne tie gardan mein daali aur aaine mein dekhte hue uski girah lagane laga.

"Kya main ise maazrat samjhu?"  Nausherwan ne bechaini se chehra uthaya.

"I'm sorry, bhai. Maine aapko bohot hurt kiya."

"Main maazrat qubool karta hoon. Bhool jao sab." Tie ki girah baandhte hue bhi woh nahi muskuraya.

"Aap mujhse abhi tak naraaz hain?"

"Nahi." Usne knot kas ke collar durust kiya, stand se coat uthaya aur mud kar Sheru ko sanjeeda nazron se dekha.

"Naraaz nahi hoon, hairan hoon. Ispe nahi ki main bewakoof kaise bana. aitbaar karne wale dhokha kha jaate hain. Ispe bhi nahi ki tum ek criminal zehniyat rakhte ho. Balki sirf ispe ki agar tumhe paise chahiye the, toh ek criminal zehniyat rakhte ho. Balki sirf ispe ki agar tumhe paise chahiye the, toh tum mere paas kyun nahi aaye?"

Baton band ki. Haath jamaya.  "Adventure karna chaah raha tha bas." Nausherwan ne sharmindagi aur khaft se gardan jhuka li. Hashim ne coat pehna aur usse dekhte hue kaha,

"Tum, Shero, meri ek baat apne dimaag mein bitha lo. tumhara bhai tumhare sab maamle sambhal sakta hai." Uske kandhe ko mazbooti se thaama. Nausherwan ne sharminda chehra uthaya.

"Tumhe paisa chahiye, tum mere paas aaoge. Tumhe koi ladki chahiye, tum mere paas aaoge. Tumhe kisi ki jaan chahiye, tum mere paas aaoge. Magar tum khud kuch nahi karoge. Kabhi bhi nahi. Samajh aaya?"  Usne asbaat mein gardan hilaai. Phir qadre jhijhka.

"Woh jo aapne kaha... ki kaash... woh.. Saadi aapka bhai hota..."

"Woh ek accha ladka hai, rishton ka ehtram karna jaanta hai... agar woh hamara teesra bhai hota toh mujhe khushi hoti... Magar woh nahi hai. Aurangzeb Kardar ke do hi bete hain. main aur tum. Tumhari nazar mein meri kitni ahmiyat hai, mujhe waqai nahi maaloom. Magar mere liye tum aur Sonia barabar ho."

"Aapko pata hai, main aapse kitni  mohabbat karta hoon? Kitna ehtram karta hoon aapka?"

"Nahi, mujhe nahi pata." Perfume khud pe chhidakte hue sanjeedgi se kandhe uchkaaye. Sheru rohaansa hi gaya.  "Yeh sach hai."

"Phir ise sabit karo. Kyunki mujhe dobara se tumhare takhrebi zehan pe aitbaar karne mein waqt lagega." Uske kandhe ko thapthapa kar Hashim bahar nikal gaya.

Nausherwan pareshaan sa khada raha.  Shehreen ab seedhiyon ke beech mein khadi thi. Use aate dekh kar raasta diya. Hashim chand zeene utara, phir uske kareeb ruka.

"Kuch kaagzat tumhare dastakhat chahiyen. Dopahar mein office aa jaana."  "Main khulaa le rahi hoon, talaaq nahi. Chaho toh yeh bhi chhod do rakam aur mara'aat na bhi do, zaroorat nahi mujhe tumhare paise ki."

"Woh baatein mat karo jinka matlab tum khud bhi nahi jaanti. Jo de raha hoon, apni beti ke liye de raha hoon. Maa se alag nahi kar raha usko. Ab hato saamne se."

Woh mazeed hati wo aage badh gaya. Shehreen tilmilate hue use jaate dekhti rahi. Aankhon mein shiddat se gussa aur bebasi thi.

Jab woh maa-baap ke kamre ke saamne ruka toh Jawahirat couch par baithi, bechaini se kaansh rahi thi, aur dressing mirror ke saamne khade Aurangzeb Mary engilo ko hidayat de rahe the.  Hashim dehleez par ruk gaya.

"Main Alicia ki fees pay kar raha hoon. Kisi ko koi aitraaz ho tab bhi mujhe kuch kehne ki zehmat na karein. Mera dimaag aaj kal bohot ghuma hua hai."    Yeh keh kar woh bina kisi aur baat ke mud gaya. Jawahirat tilmila kar uthi, Aurangzeb ne use barhami se pukara, magar woh bahar ja chuka tha. Dono bebasi se ek doosre ko dekhte reh gaye.  Bahar dhund abhi tak chhayi thi. Woh baraamde tak pahuncha hi tha jab Khawar tez raftaar se uski taraf aata dikha.

"Saadi Yousuf ne aapke kiye wakeel ko fire kar diya hai!"

"Maloom hai."

"Aap itne befikar kaise ho sakte hain?" "Fikar ki kya baat hai?" Hashim ulta hairan hua. "Log wakeel badalte rehte hain. Agla bhi hamara hi hoga, nahi toh judge toh hamara hi hai."

"Magar mujhe pareshaani hai... un logon ko woh audio kahan se mili?"

"Kaunsi audio?" Hashim thathak ruka. Khawar ne Mahmood sahab se jo suna tha, bata diya.

"Haan, Zumar aise kaam kar sakti hai. Woh keh raha hai toh aisa hi hoga." Hashim apni gaadi ki taraf badh raha tha. Khawar tez uske peeche bhaga.  "Kya waqai aisa hi hai? Ho sakta hai woh jhoot bol raha ho."  Hashim ruka aur use dekhne laga.

"Aapne use aur uski behan ko us raat apna laptop diya tha. Kahin usne wahaan se toh nahi nikaali?"

"Aisa kuch nahi hai." Hashim bezar ho gaya. "Woh audio mere safe mein hai. Maine do din pehle hi dekhi hai. Laptop mein mere documents ka folder locked hai. Woh dono itne bhi smart nahi hain ke sab kuch khol sakein. Aur Saadi jhoot nahi bolta. Jo keh raha hai, wahi hoga. Magar judge hamara hai, phir kya masla hai?"

Khawar ruk gaya. "Sir, aapka over confidence..." Kehte kehte ruka.  Hashim ne ek sakht, kaat daar nazar us par daali aur aage badh gaya.

Khawar ne bechaini se thodhi khujaayi.  Bajaahir Hashim sahi keh raha tha, magar phir bhi use kuch gadbad mehsoos ho rahi thi. Khair Hashim Saadi ko behtar jaanta tha yaqeenan!  Wo sar jhatakta aage badh gaya.
